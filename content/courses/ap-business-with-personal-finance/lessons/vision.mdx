| Statement | Answers | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vision | Where are we going? | A world where every student can learn to code |
| mission | What do we do now, for whom? | We provide free coding lessons to middle schools |
| values | How do we behave? | Integrity, inclusion, curiosity |
| goals | What will we achieve, by when? | Reach 200 schools by June |

**SMART** goals turn the vision into measurable steps that can be tracked with KPIs.

<Note title="Alignment">
Strategies and day-to-day decisions should be consistent with the mission and move the business toward its vision.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Make it SMART">
Rewrite 'get more customers' as a SMART goal for a café.

1. Make it specific and measurable, with a deadline.

**Answer:** Increase average weekday customers from 120 to 150 by the end of March.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** A purchasing manager awards a contract to a company owned by her brother without disclosing the relationship. What is the ethical problem?

**A.** A conflict of interest. Personal interests influenced a business decision.

**Q.** Which organizational structure has few management levels and wide spans of control?

**A.** A flat structure. Flat structures have few layers.

**Q.** A company sells the lowest-priced basic laptops by producing at huge scale. What competitive strategy is this?

**A.** Cost leadership. It competes on low cost and price.

**Q.** Which goal is SMART?

**A.** Increase online sales from $40,000 to $50,000 per month by December 31. It is specific, measurable and time-bound.

**Q.** Two founders want limited personal liability and simple pass-through taxation. Which form of business best fits?

**A.** A limited liability company (LLC). LLCs offer limited liability with flexible, simple taxation.

**Q.** Which department is mainly responsible for recruiting, training and benefits?

**A.** Human resources. HR manages people.

**Q.** Which is a vision statement rather than a mission statement?

**A.** "A world where every family has access to fresh, affordable food.". Vision describes the long-term future the organization wants.

**Q.** A student notices that classmates struggle to find quiet study spaces and starts an app listing open rooms. Where did this business idea come from?

**A.** Identifying a problem experienced by a group of customers. Many ideas start from an unmet need.
