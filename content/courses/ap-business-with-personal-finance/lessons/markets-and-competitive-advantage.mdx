A **market** is the set of buyers and sellers of a product. The more competitors and the more similar their products, the more intense the competition.

## Competitive advantage

- **Cost leadership**: produce at lower cost and compete on price.
- **Differentiation**: offer something customers value and rivals do not (quality, design, service, brand).
- **Focus**: target a narrow market segment with either strategy.

$$\text{market share} = \frac{\text{company sales}}{\text{total market sales}} \times 100\%$$

Sustainable advantages are hard to copy: patents, brand loyalty, unique know-how, scale.

<Note title="Advantage must matter to customers">
A difference only creates advantage if target customers value it enough to choose the product.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Identify the strategy">
A streaming service charges more than rivals but offers exclusive live sports. What strategy is this?

1. It competes on a feature rivals lack, not on price.

**Answer:** differentiation.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Annual sales in the market ($ millions)*

| Company | Sales |
| --- | --- |
| Lumen | 110 |
| Orbio | 145 |
| Northline | 45 |
| Kinetica | 55 |

What is Northline's market share by revenue?

**Answer:** 12.7%

**Why:** Company sales divided by total market sales.

**Working:** 45 ÷ 355 = 12.7%.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 14.5%: Divide by the total market, including the company itself.
- 31%: Divide by the market total, not the leader's sales.
- 25%: Shares are based on sales, not the number of companies.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** A purchasing manager awards a contract to a company owned by her brother without disclosing the relationship. What is the ethical problem?

**A.** A conflict of interest. Personal interests influenced a business decision.

**Q.** Which organizational structure has few management levels and wide spans of control?

**A.** A flat structure. Flat structures have few layers.

**Q.** A company sells the lowest-priced basic laptops by producing at huge scale. What competitive strategy is this?

**A.** Cost leadership. It competes on low cost and price.

**Q.** Which goal is SMART?

**A.** Increase online sales from $40,000 to $50,000 per month by December 31. It is specific, measurable and time-bound.

**Q.** Two founders want limited personal liability and simple pass-through taxation. Which form of business best fits?

**A.** A limited liability company (LLC). LLCs offer limited liability with flexible, simple taxation.

**Q.** Which department is mainly responsible for recruiting, training and benefits?

**A.** Human resources. HR manages people.

**Q.** Which is a vision statement rather than a mission statement?

**A.** "A world where every family has access to fresh, affordable food.". Vision describes the long-term future the organization wants.

**Q.** A student notices that classmates struggle to find quiet study spaces and starts an app listing open rooms. Where did this business idea come from?

**A.** Identifying a problem experienced by a group of customers. Many ideas start from an unmet need.

### Free-response practice: Brightwave enters the market

A media company plans to launch Brightwave, a streaming service, in a market with four established rivals. Its executives reviewed industry publications and the rivals' public financial reports.

*Rival streaming services*

| Service | Market share by revenue (%) | Monthly price ($) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NetCom | 11 | 14.99 |
| OrbioTV | 28 | 14.99 |
| VistaFlix | 30 | 15.99 |
| Kinovo | 31 | 15.99 |

**(a)** Describe the market research method the executives used.

To earn the point: Secondary research. Model answer: They used existing data from publications and published financial statements, not data they collected themselves.

**(b)** Describe one finding revealed by the research.

To earn the point: An accurate finding from the data. Model answer: Kinovo leads with 31% of revenue; NetCom is the cheapest at $14.99.

**(c)** Explain how Brightwave could use a finding to achieve a business goal, such as gaining market share.

To earn the point: Connects a finding to a strategy and goal. Model answer: Because the market is concentrated and prices range from $14.99 to $15.99, Brightwave could use penetration pricing below $14.99 or differentiate with exclusive content to win share.
