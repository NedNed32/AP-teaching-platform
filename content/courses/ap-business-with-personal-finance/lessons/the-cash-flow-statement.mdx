| Section | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| operating | cash from customers, payments to suppliers and employees |
| investing | buying or selling equipment and property |
| financing | loans received or repaid, owner investment, dividends |

$$\text{ending cash} = \text{beginning cash} + \text{net cash flow}$$

Cash flow problems are a leading cause of small-business failure. Businesses manage cash by collecting receivables faster, negotiating supplier terms, keeping reserves, and using lines of credit.

<Note title="Nonprofits too">
Nonprofits must manage cash flow carefully when donations or grants arrive at different times from expenses.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Ending cash">
A business begins March with $8,000. Operating cash flow is +$5,000, it buys a $6,000 oven, and repays $1,500 of a loan. Ending cash?

1. 8,000 + 5,000 − 6,000 − 1,500.

**Answer:** $5,500.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A business starts the month with $26,000 in cash. Operating activities bring in $0, it spends $8,000 on new equipment, and financing activities net $2,500. What is its ending cash balance?

**Answer:** $20,500

**Why:** Ending cash = beginning cash + operating + investing + financing.

**Working:** $26,000 + $0 − $8,000 + $2,500 = $20,500.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $36,500: Buying equipment is a cash outflow.
- $26,000: Include investing and financing flows.
- −$5,500: Add the beginning balance.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** What does the accounting equation state?

**A.** Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity. Everything a business owns is financed by debt or owners.

**Q.** In which section of the cash flow statement does repaying a bank loan appear?

**A.** Financing activities. Borrowing and repaying loans are financing.

**Q.** Which internal control best prevents one employee from both stealing cash and hiding it in the records?

**A.** Separation of duties. Different people handle cash and record-keeping.

**Q.** Why does paying only the minimum on a credit card balance often cost so much?

**A.** Interest continues to accrue on the remaining balance, stretching repayment over years. Most of a minimum payment goes to interest.

**Q.** A founder wants to raise money without giving up any ownership. Which source fits?

**A.** A bank loan. Debt does not dilute ownership.

**Q.** A company is profitable on its income statement but cannot pay its suppliers this month. What is the most likely explanation?

**A.** Customers have not yet paid, so cash inflows lag behind recorded sales. Profit is not the same as cash.

**Q.** Which factor has the greatest effect on a person's credit score?

**A.** Payment history. On-time payments matter most.
