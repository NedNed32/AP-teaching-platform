## Loan payments

$$M = P\frac{i(1+i)^n}{(1+i)^n - 1}$$

where $$i$$ is the monthly rate and $$n$$ the number of payments. Total interest = $$Mn - P$$.

## Credit scores

Payment history, credit utilization (balance ÷ limit, ideally low), length of history, new credit, and credit mix. Good credit lowers borrowing costs and can affect renting and insurance.

<Note title="Good and bad debt">
Borrowing can build assets (education, a home) or finance consumption. Compare the cost of borrowing with the benefit.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Credit utilization">
A borrower has $1,800 of balances on cards with a combined $6,000 limit. What is their utilization?

1. 1,800 ÷ 6,000 = 0.30.

**Answer:** 30%.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

Lena has two credit cards. Card 1 has a $5,000 limit and a $2,200 balance. Card 2 has a $3,500 limit and a $200 balance. What is the overall credit utilization rate?

**Answer:** 28.2%

**Why:** Total balances ÷ total limits.

**Working:** ($2,200 + $200) ÷ ($5,000 + $3,500) = 28.2%.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 24.9%: Combine totals rather than averaging the two rates.
- 48%: Use the combined limit.
- 71.8%: That is the unused share of credit.

### Worked exam question 2

A $9,000 car loan at 6% APR is repaid in 60 equal monthly payments of $174.00. How much interest is paid over the life of the loan?

**Answer:** $1,439.71

**Why:** Total paid (payment × number of payments) minus the amount borrowed.

**Working:** $174.00 × 60 = $10,439.71; minus $9,000 = $1,439.71.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $2,700.00: That is simple interest on the full amount; the balance falls as you pay.
- $10,439.71: That is the total repaid, including principal.
- $540.00: That is only one year of interest on the original balance.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** What does the accounting equation state?

**A.** Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity. Everything a business owns is financed by debt or owners.

**Q.** In which section of the cash flow statement does repaying a bank loan appear?

**A.** Financing activities. Borrowing and repaying loans are financing.

**Q.** Which internal control best prevents one employee from both stealing cash and hiding it in the records?

**A.** Separation of duties. Different people handle cash and record-keeping.

**Q.** Why does paying only the minimum on a credit card balance often cost so much?

**A.** Interest continues to accrue on the remaining balance, stretching repayment over years. Most of a minimum payment goes to interest.

**Q.** A founder wants to raise money without giving up any ownership. Which source fits?

**A.** A bank loan. Debt does not dilute ownership.

**Q.** A company is profitable on its income statement but cannot pay its suppliers this month. What is the most likely explanation?

**A.** Customers have not yet paid, so cash inflows lag behind recorded sales. Profit is not the same as cash.

**Q.** Which factor has the greatest effect on a person's credit score?

**A.** Payment history. On-time payments matter most.
