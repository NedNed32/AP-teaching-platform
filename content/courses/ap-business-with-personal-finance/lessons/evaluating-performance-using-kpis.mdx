| Area | Example KPIs |
| --- | --- |
| customers | retention rate, satisfaction score, conversion rate |
| market | market share, sales growth |
| operations | units per hour, defect rate, delivery time |
| finance | profit margin, cash flow, return on investment |

$$\text{growth rate} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \times 100\% \qquad \text{ROI} = \frac{\text{gain} - \text{cost}}{\text{cost}} \times 100\%$$

Good KPIs are tied to goals, measurable, and compared with **benchmarks** (past performance, targets or competitors).

<Note title="Measure what matters">
Tracking too many numbers hides the important ones. Choose a few KPIs linked to the strategy.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Retention">
A gym starts the year with 400 members and keeps 340 of them (ignoring new members). What is its retention rate?

1. 340 ÷ 400.

**Answer:** 85%.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A business's quarterly revenue changed from 199,000 to 284,500. What is the percentage change?

**Answer:** 43%

**Why:** (New − old) ÷ old.

**Working:** (284500 − 199000) ÷ 199000 = 43%.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 30.1%: Divide by the original value.
- 143%: Subtract 1 (or subtract old from new first).
- -43%: Check the direction of the change.

### Worked exam question 2

Option A costs $27,000 and saves $9,000 per year. Option B costs $13,000 and saves $6,500 per year. Based on payback period alone, which option should the business choose?

**Answer:** Option B, with a payback of 2.0 years

**Why:** Payback period = cost ÷ annual savings; shorter is better.

**Working:** A: $27,000 ÷ $9,000 = 3.0 years. B: $13,000 ÷ $6,500 = 2.0 years.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Option A, with a payback of 3.0 years: The shorter payback is preferred.
- Option A, because it saves more each year: Payback also depends on the cost.
- Option B, because it costs less: Payback depends on savings as well as cost.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the four functions of management involves comparing results with goals and correcting course?

**A.** Controlling. Controlling measures and adjusts performance.

**Q.** During a fire in a warehouse, a manager gives direct orders without consulting the team. Which leadership style fits this situation?

**A.** Autocratic. Emergencies need fast, clear decisions.

**Q.** A café wants to measure how well it keeps customers coming back. Which KPI fits best?

**A.** Customer retention rate. It measures repeat customers.

**Q.** Before comparing two strategic options, why should a business define its decision criteria?

**A.** So the options are judged consistently against what matters most to the business. Criteria make the comparison objective.

**Q.** Many companies can supply the plain cardboard boxes a business needs, and switching between them is easy. According to Porter's Five Forces, supplier power is:

**A.** Low. Many suppliers and easy switching weaken supplier power.

**Q.** Customers can easily switch from a company's energy drink to coffee or tea. Which of Porter's Five Forces is strong?

**A.** Threat of substitutes. Other products meet the same need.

### Free-response practice: Harbor Outfitters chooses between two options

Harbor Outfitters has funds for one project. **Option A**: add a premium product line, costing $48,000 and expected to add $12,000 in annual profit. It would also require the owner to manage a new team. **Option B**: expand advertising to a new customer segment, costing $16,000 and expected to add $3,000 in annual profit, with more uncertainty because the business has less experience in this area. The owner's goals are to grow profit, keep risk manageable, and maintain the business's reputation for customer service.

**(a)** Establish two decision-making criteria the business should use, and explain why each is appropriate given its goals.

To earn the point: Two relevant criteria tied to the goals. Model answer: Financial return (payback or ROI) and risk; or impact on customer service.

**(b)** Compare the two options using your criteria and the data. Include at least one calculation.

To earn the point: Payback periods. Model answer: A: $48,000 ÷ $12,000 = 4.0 years. B: $16,000 ÷ $3,000 = 5.3 years.

To earn the point: Three-year ROI or another valid comparison. Model answer: A: -25%; B: -43.8%.

To earn the point: Compares nonfinancial criteria. Model answer: A demands management time; B carries more uncertainty from inexperience.

**(c)** Make a recommendation and support it with reasoning and evidence from the scenario. Acknowledge a drawback of your choice.

To earn the point: Clear recommendation consistent with the analysis. Model answer: Choose Option A for its faster payback (4.0 years), if the owner can manage the drawback.

To earn the point: Acknowledges a trade-off. Model answer: Option A ties up more capital and needs management time.
