| Line | Example |
| --- | --- |
| revenue | 500,000 |
| − cost of goods sold | 300,000 |
| = gross profit | 200,000 |
| − operating expenses | 140,000 |
| = operating income | 60,000 |
| − interest and taxes | 15,000 |
| = net income | 45,000 |

$$\text{gross margin} = \frac{\text{gross profit}}{\text{revenue}} \qquad \text{net margin} = \frac{\text{net income}}{\text{revenue}}$$

In the example, the gross margin is 40% and the net margin 9%.

<Note title="Comparing periods">
Margins make it possible to compare businesses of different sizes or the same business across years.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Net income">
Revenue $80,000; COGS $35,000; operating expenses $30,000; taxes $3,000. Find net income and net margin.

1. Gross profit 45,000; operating income 15,000; net income 12,000.
2. 12,000 ÷ 80,000 = 15%.

**Answer:** $12,000, a 15% net margin.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Maple Street Bakery income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $113,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $42,000 |
| Operating expenses | $25,000 |
| Taxes | $9,200 |

What is the business's net profit margin?

**Answer:** 32.6%

**Why:** Net income ÷ revenue.

**Working:** Net income $36,800 ÷ $113,000 = 32.6%.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 62.8%: That is the gross margin.
- 40.7%: Subtract taxes too.
- 87.6%: Divide by revenue.

### Worked exam question 2

*Harbor Outfitters income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $177,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $99,000 |
| Operating expenses | $56,500 |
| Taxes | $4,300 |

What is the business's gross profit?

**Answer:** $78,000

**Why:** Revenue − cost of goods sold.

**Working:** Gross profit $78,000; operating income $21,500; net income $17,200.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $21,500: Gross profit subtracts only COGS.
- $17,200: That is net income.
- $120,500: Subtract COGS, not operating expenses.

### Worked exam question 3

*Nimbus Apps income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $49,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $29,500 |
| Operating expenses | $11,000 |
| Taxes | $1,700 |

What is the business's net income?

**Answer:** $6,800

**Why:** Revenue − COGS − operating expenses − taxes.

**Working:** Gross profit $19,500; operating income $8,500; net income $6,800.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $19,500: That is gross profit.
- $8,500: Subtract taxes too.
- $10,200: Taxes are subtracted.
