## Insurance basics

Health, auto, renters or homeowners, life and disability insurance. Key terms: **premium**, **deductible**, **copay**, **coverage limit**.

A higher deductible lowers premiums but raises out-of-pocket costs when a claim happens.

Protect against **identity theft** by guarding personal information, monitoring accounts and credit reports, and using strong authentication.

<Note title="Insure big, save for small">
Insurance is most valuable for losses you could not afford; small losses can be covered by an emergency fund.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Out-of-pocket cost">
A car repair costs $2,600. The policy has a $500 deductible. How much does insurance pay?

1. 2,600 − 500.

**Answer:** $2,100 (the owner pays $500).
</WorkedExample>
