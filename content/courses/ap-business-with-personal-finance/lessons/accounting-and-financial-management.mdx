Accounting records, summarizes and reports financial information so people can make decisions.

$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Owner's Equity}$$

| Statement | Shows | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| income statement | revenue, expenses, profit | over a period |
| balance sheet | assets, liabilities, equity | at a point in time |
| cash flow statement | cash in and out | over a period |

**Financial management** uses this information for budgeting, pricing, investing and financing decisions.

<Note title="Profit is not cash">
A business can be profitable on paper and still run out of cash if customers pay late.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Use the equation">
A business has $40,000 in equity and $25,000 in liabilities. What are its total assets?

1. Assets = 25,000 + 40,000.

**Answer:** $65,000.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Balance sheet items*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | $22,000 |
| Inventory | $28,000 |
| Equipment | $65,000 |
| Current liabilities | $36,000 |
| Long-term loan | $28,000 |

What is the business's current ratio?

**Answer:** 1.39

**Why:** Current assets (cash + inventory) ÷ current liabilities.

**Working:** ($22,000 + $28,000) ÷ $36,000 = 1.39.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 3.19: Equipment is not a current asset.
- 0.72: The ratio is inverted.
- 0.78: Use only current liabilities.

### Worked exam question 2

*Balance sheet items*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Cash | $56,000 |
| Inventory | $14,000 |
| Equipment | $73,000 |
| Current liabilities | $23,000 |
| Long-term loan | $7,000 |

What is the owner's equity?

**Answer:** $113,000

**Why:** Equity = assets − liabilities.

**Working:** Assets $143,000 − liabilities $30,000 = $113,000.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $143,000: Subtract the liabilities.
- $120,000: Subtract the long-term loan too.
- $173,000: Liabilities are subtracted.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** What does the accounting equation state?

**A.** Assets = Liabilities + Owner's equity. Everything a business owns is financed by debt or owners.

**Q.** In which section of the cash flow statement does repaying a bank loan appear?

**A.** Financing activities. Borrowing and repaying loans are financing.

**Q.** Which internal control best prevents one employee from both stealing cash and hiding it in the records?

**A.** Separation of duties. Different people handle cash and record-keeping.

**Q.** Why does paying only the minimum on a credit card balance often cost so much?

**A.** Interest continues to accrue on the remaining balance, stretching repayment over years. Most of a minimum payment goes to interest.

**Q.** A founder wants to raise money without giving up any ownership. Which source fits?

**A.** A bank loan. Debt does not dilute ownership.

**Q.** A company is profitable on its income statement but cannot pay its suppliers this month. What is the most likely explanation?

**A.** Customers have not yet paid, so cash inflows lag behind recorded sales. Profit is not the same as cash.

**Q.** Which factor has the greatest effect on a person's credit score?

**A.** Payment history. On-time payments matter most.
