## Management functions

1. **Planning**: setting goals and strategies.
2. **Organizing**: arranging people and resources.
3. **Leading**: motivating and communicating.
4. **Controlling**: measuring performance and correcting course.

## Leadership styles

| Style | Description | Fits when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| autocratic | leader decides alone | crisis, inexperienced team |
| democratic | team input | complex problems, engaged team |
| delegative (laissez-faire) | team decides | experts, high trust |
| transformational | inspires change | growth, innovation |

Effective leaders adapt their style to the situation and the team.

<Note title="Motivation">
Pay matters, but recognition, growth, autonomy and meaningful work also motivate employees.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Choose a style">
A team of experienced software engineers is designing a new feature. Which style likely works best?

1. They are experts who benefit from autonomy.

**Answer:** delegative or democratic.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the four functions of management involves comparing results with goals and correcting course?

**A.** Controlling. Controlling measures and adjusts performance.

**Q.** During a fire in a warehouse, a manager gives direct orders without consulting the team. Which leadership style fits this situation?

**A.** Autocratic. Emergencies need fast, clear decisions.

**Q.** A café wants to measure how well it keeps customers coming back. Which KPI fits best?

**A.** Customer retention rate. It measures repeat customers.

**Q.** Before comparing two strategic options, why should a business define its decision criteria?

**A.** So the options are judged consistently against what matters most to the business. Criteria make the comparison objective.

**Q.** Many companies can supply the plain cardboard boxes a business needs, and switching between them is easy. According to Porter's Five Forces, supplier power is:

**A.** Low. Many suppliers and easy switching weaken supplier power.

**Q.** Customers can easily switch from a company's energy drink to coffee or tea. Which of Porter's Five Forces is strong?

**A.** Threat of substitutes. Other products meet the same need.
