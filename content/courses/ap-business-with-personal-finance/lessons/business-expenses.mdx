$$\text{total cost} = \text{fixed costs} + \text{variable cost per unit} \times \text{quantity}$$

| Fixed | Variable |
| --- | --- |
| rent, insurance, salaries | materials, packaging, shipping, sales commissions |

**Average cost** per unit falls as output rises because fixed costs are spread over more units (economies of scale).

Expenses on the income statement include COGS and **operating expenses** (marketing, administration, rent).

<Note title="Controlling costs">
Lowering variable cost per unit improves every sale's contribution; lowering fixed costs lowers the break-even point.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Average cost">
Fixed costs are $12,000 and variable cost is $3 per unit. What is the average cost per unit at 2,000 units?

1. Total cost = 12,000 + 3(2,000) = 18,000.
2. 18,000 ÷ 2,000.

**Answer:** $9 per unit.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Maple Street Bakery income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $113,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $42,000 |
| Operating expenses | $25,000 |
| Taxes | $9,200 |

What is the business's net profit margin?

**Answer:** 32.6%

**Why:** Net income ÷ revenue.

**Working:** Net income $36,800 ÷ $113,000 = 32.6%.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 62.8%: That is the gross margin.
- 40.7%: Subtract taxes too.
- 87.6%: Divide by revenue.

### Worked exam question 2

*Harbor Outfitters income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $177,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $99,000 |
| Operating expenses | $56,500 |
| Taxes | $4,300 |

What is the business's gross profit?

**Answer:** $78,000

**Why:** Revenue − cost of goods sold.

**Working:** Gross profit $78,000; operating income $21,500; net income $17,200.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $21,500: Gross profit subtracts only COGS.
- $17,200: That is net income.
- $120,500: Subtract COGS, not operating expenses.

### Worked exam question 3

*Nimbus Apps income statement*

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue | $49,000 |
| Cost of goods sold | $29,500 |
| Operating expenses | $11,000 |
| Taxes | $1,700 |

What is the business's net income?

**Answer:** $6,800

**Why:** Revenue − COGS − operating expenses − taxes.

**Working:** Gross profit $19,500; operating income $8,500; net income $6,800.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $19,500: That is gross profit.
- $8,500: Subtract taxes too.
- $10,200: Taxes are subtracted.

### Worked exam question 5

A business has fixed costs of $40,000 and a variable cost of $11 per unit. What is its average cost per unit when it produces 6,300 units?

**Answer:** $17.35

**Why:** (Fixed costs + variable cost × quantity) ÷ quantity.

**Working:** ($40,000 + $11 × 6300) ÷ 6300 = $17.35.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $11.00: Include the fixed costs spread across units.
- $6.35: Add the variable cost per unit.
- $109,300.00: That is total cost; divide by quantity.
