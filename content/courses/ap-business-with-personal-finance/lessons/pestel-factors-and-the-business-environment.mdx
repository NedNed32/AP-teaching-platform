| Factor | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Political | trade policy, tariffs, government stability |
| Economic | interest rates, inflation, unemployment, income |
| Social | demographics, trends, values, lifestyles |
| Technological | new tools, automation, digital platforms |
| Environmental | climate, sustainability expectations, resources |
| Legal | regulations, employment law, consumer protection |

PESTEL factors are **external**: the business cannot control them but must respond. They can create **opportunities** or **threats** (they feed the O and T of a SWOT analysis).

<Note title="Political or legal?">
Political covers government priorities and policy direction; legal covers the specific laws that result.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Classify">
Growing consumer interest in plant-based diets helps a vegan food startup. Which PESTEL factor is this?

1. It reflects changing preferences and lifestyles.

**Answer:** Social.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Growing interest in plant-based diets increases demand for meat alternatives. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Social. It concerns social conditions in the business environment. Social.

**Q.** An aging population increases demand for home health services. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Social. It concerns social conditions in the business environment. Social.

**Q.** The central bank raises interest rates, making business loans more expensive. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Economic. It concerns economic conditions in the business environment. Economic.

**Q.** More frequent droughts reduce the supply of locally grown produce. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Environmental. It concerns environmental conditions in the business environment. Environmental.

**Q.** Advances in battery technology lower the cost of electric delivery vans. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Technological. It concerns technological conditions in the business environment. Technological.

**Q.** The government announces subsidies for solar panel installation. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Political. It concerns political conditions in the business environment. Political.

**Q.** A new law requires apps to get parental consent before collecting data from children. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Legal. It concerns legal conditions in the business environment. Legal.

**Q.** Customers increasingly expect sustainable packaging. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Environmental. It concerns environmental conditions in the business environment. Environmental.

**Q.** A new trade agreement removes tariffs on imported parts. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Political. It concerns political conditions in the business environment. Political.

**Q.** Inflation raises the cost of raw materials. Which PESTEL factor does this represent?

**A.** Economic. It concerns economic conditions in the business environment. Economic.
