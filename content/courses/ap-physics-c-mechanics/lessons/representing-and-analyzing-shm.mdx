$$x = A\cos(\omega t + \phi) \qquad v = -A\omega\sin(\omega t + \phi) \qquad a = -A\omega^2\cos(\omega t + \phi)$$

- Maximum speed $$v_{max} = A\omega$$ at equilibrium.
- Maximum acceleration $$a_{max} = A\omega^2$$ at the turning points.
- Acceleration is always opposite to displacement.

On graphs, velocity is a quarter cycle ahead of position, and acceleration is the mirror image of position.

<Note title="Reading phase">
If x = A at t = 0, φ = 0 (cosine). If x = 0 and moving in the positive direction at t = 0, φ = −π/2 (a sine curve).
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Maximum values">
x = 0.05 cos(10t) m. Find v_max and a_max.

1. $$v_{max} = 0.05(10) = 0.50$$ m/s.
2. $$a_{max} = 0.05(100) = 5.0$$ m/s².

**Answer:** 0.50 m/s and 5.0 m/s².
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

An oscillator's position is $$x(t) = 0.09\cos(15t)$$ m. What is its maximum speed?

**Answer:** $$1.35\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

**Why:** v_max = Aω.

**Working:** v_max = Aω = 1.35 m/s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$20.2\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: That is a_max.
- $$0.09\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Multiply by ω.
- $$167\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: v_max = Aω.

### Worked exam question 2

An oscillator's position is $$x(t) = 0.12\cos(3t)$$ m. What is its maximum acceleration?

**Answer:** $$1.08\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$$

**Why:** a_max = Aω².

**Working:** a_max = Aω² = 1.08 m/s².

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$0.36\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$$: That is v_max.
- $$3.24\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$$: Differentiate twice: Aω².
- $$9\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$$: Multiply by the amplitude.

### Free-response practice: A mass on a spring

A block of mass 0.5 kg on a horizontal frictionless surface is attached to a spring. Its position is $$x(t) = 0.06\cos(7t)$$, with x in meters and t in seconds.

**(a)** Sketch (describe) graphs of position, velocity and acceleration versus time for one period, aligned vertically.

To earn the point: Position is a cosine starting at +A; velocity is a negative sine starting at 0. Model answer: x starts at 0.06 m; v starts at 0 and goes negative.

To earn the point: Acceleration starts at its negative maximum and is opposite in sign to position throughout. Model answer: $$a = -\omega^2 x$$

**(b)** Derive expressions for v(t) and a(t), and determine the maximum speed and maximum acceleration.

To earn the point: Correct derivatives. Model answer: $$v = -0.42\sin(7t)$$, $$a = -2.94\cos(7t)$$

To earn the point: Correct maxima. Model answer: v_max = 0.42 m/s; a_max = 2.94 m/s²

**(c)** Calculate the spring constant and the period.

To earn the point: Spring constant. Model answer: k = mω² = 24.5 N/m

To earn the point: Period. Model answer: T = 2π/ω = 0.898 s

**(d)** Calculate the total mechanical energy and describe how kinetic and potential energy vary during one period.

To earn the point: Total energy. Model answer: E = ½kA² = 0.0441 J

To earn the point: Energy exchange. Model answer: K is maximum at x = 0 and zero at x = ±A; U is the reverse; the sum is constant. Each varies twice per period.
