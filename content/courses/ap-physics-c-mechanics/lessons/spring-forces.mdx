$$F_s = -k\Delta x$$

The negative sign shows the force points back toward equilibrium. The spring constant $$k$$ (N/m) measures stiffness: the slope of a force-versus-stretch graph.

| Arrangement | Equivalent constant |
| --- | --- |
| parallel | $$k_{eq} = k_1 + k_2$$ |
| series | $$\frac{1}{k_{eq}} = \frac{1}{k_1} + \frac{1}{k_2}$$ |

A mass hanging at rest on a spring stretches it by $$mg/k$$.

<Note title="Measuring k">
Hang different masses, measure the stretch, and graph force against stretch. The slope of the best-fit line is k.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Hanging mass">
A 0.50 kg mass stretches a spring 0.12 m at equilibrium. Find k.

1. $$k = mg/x = (0.50)(9.8)/0.12$$.

**Answer:** about 41 N/m.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

Two springs with constants 80 N/m and 50 N/m are connected in parallel and support a 0.5 kg mass at rest. How far do they stretch in total?

**Answer:** $$0.0377\ \mathrm{m}$$

**Why:** k_eq = k₁ + k₂.

**Working:** k_eq = 130 N/m; x = mg/k_eq = 0.0377 m.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$0.159\ \mathrm{m}$$: Series and parallel rules are reversed.
- $$0.0613\ \mathrm{m}$$: Both springs share the load.
- $$3.85 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m}$$: The force is the weight, mg.

### Worked exam question 2

A spring with $$k = 390$$ N/m is compressed 0.08 m and launches a 0.7 kg block across a frictionless surface. What is the block's speed after leaving the spring?

**Answer:** $$1.89\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

**Why:** ½kx² = ½mv².

**Working:** v = x√(k/m) = 0.08√(390/0.7) = 1.89 m/s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$44.6\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Use energy: v = x√(k/m).
- $$2.67\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: The ½ appears on both sides.
- $$3.57\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Take the square root.
