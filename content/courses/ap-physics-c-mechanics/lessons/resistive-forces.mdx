For a falling object with drag $$-bv$$:

$$m\frac{dv}{dt} = mg - bv$$

Separating variables and integrating with $$v(0) = 0$$:

$$v(t) = \frac{mg}{b}\left(1 - e^{-bt/m}\right)$$

The **terminal velocity** is $$v_T = \frac{mg}{b}$$ and the time constant is $$\tau = \frac{m}{b}$$. Acceleration starts at $$g$$ and decays exponentially to zero.

For an object slowed only by drag (horizontal motion), $$v = v_0e^{-bt/m}$$.

<Note title="Terminal velocity without solving">
At terminal velocity the acceleration is zero, so set the net force to zero: mg = bv.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Terminal speed">
A 0.20 kg object falls with drag force bv, b = 0.050 kg/s. Find v_T and τ.

1. $$v_T = (0.20)(9.8)/0.050 = 39$$ m/s.
2. $$\tau = 0.20/0.050 = 4.0$$ s.

**Answer:** 39 m/s and 4.0 s.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

An object of mass 0.8 kg falls from rest with a drag force $$F = -bv$$, where $$b = 0.01$$ kg/s. What is its speed at $$t = m/b$$?

**Answer:** $$496\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

**Why:** v = v_T(1 − e^(−t/τ)) and t = τ.

**Working:** v_T = mg/b = 784 m/s and τ = m/b = 80 s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$288\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: v = v_T(1 − e^(−1)).
- $$392\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: The approach is exponential.
- $$784\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Terminal speed is reached only as t → ∞.

### Worked exam question 2

An object of mass 1.3 kg falls from rest with a drag force $$F = -bv$$, where $$b = 0.15$$ kg/s. What is the time constant of its approach to terminal speed?

**Answer:** $$8.67\ \mathrm{s}$$

**Why:** From m dv/dt = mg − bv, τ = m/b.

**Working:** v_T = mg/b = 84.9 m/s and τ = m/b = 8.67 s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$0.115\ \mathrm{s}$$: τ = m/b.
- $$84.9\ \mathrm{s}$$: That is the terminal speed.
- $$1.91\ \mathrm{s}$$: τ has units of seconds: m/b.

### Worked exam question 3

An object of mass 0.6 kg falls from rest with a drag force $$F = -bv$$, where $$b = 0.01$$ kg/s. What is its terminal speed?

**Answer:** $$588\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

**Why:** At terminal speed, mg = bv.

**Working:** v_T = mg/b = 588 m/s and τ = m/b = 60 s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$1.7 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Solve mg = bv for v.
- $$60\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: That is the time constant.
- $$24.2\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Drag here is linear in v.
