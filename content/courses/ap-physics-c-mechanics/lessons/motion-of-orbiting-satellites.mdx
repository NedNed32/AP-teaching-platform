## Circular orbits

$$\frac{GMm}{r^2} = \frac{mv^2}{r} \Rightarrow v = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} \qquad T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3$$

Energy: $$K = \frac{GMm}{2r}$$, $$U = -\frac{GMm}{r}$$, $$E = -\frac{GMm}{2r}$$.

## Elliptical orbits

Both energy and angular momentum (about the central body) are conserved: $$mv_1r_1 = mv_2r_2$$ at the closest and farthest points.

**Escape speed**: $$v_{esc} = \sqrt{2GM/R}$$.

<Note title="Bound orbits">
A negative total energy means the satellite is bound; zero or positive total energy means it escapes.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Period ratio">
Satellite B orbits at 4 times the radius of satellite A. Compare their periods.

1. $$T \propto r^{3/2}$$.
2. $$4^{3/2} = 8$$.

**Answer:** B's period is 8 times A's.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** Satellite B orbits a planet in a circular orbit 16 times the radius of satellite A's circular orbit. What is the ratio of B's orbital speed to A's?

**A.** $$0.25$$. v = √(GM/r), so v ∝ r^(−1/2). v_B/v_A = 1/√16 = 0.25.
