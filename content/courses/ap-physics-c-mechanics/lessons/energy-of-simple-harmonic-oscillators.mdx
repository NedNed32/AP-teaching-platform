$$E = \tfrac12 kA^2 = \tfrac12 mv^2 + \tfrac12 kx^2$$

Energy sloshes between kinetic and potential; their sum stays constant.

$$v(x) = \omega\sqrt{A^2 - x^2}$$

Kinetic and potential energy each oscillate at **twice** the frequency of the position.

<Note title="Damping">
Friction or drag removes energy, so the amplitude decreases over time.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Speed at a position">
A mass on a spring (ω = 4.0 rad/s) has A = 0.10 m. Find its speed at x = 0.06 m.

1. $$v = 4.0\sqrt{0.0100 - 0.0036} = 4.0(0.080)$$.

**Answer:** 0.32 m/s.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A mass on a spring oscillates with angular frequency 9 rad/s and amplitude 0.13 m. What is its speed when it is 0.052 m from equilibrium?

**Answer:** $$1.07\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

**Why:** v = ω√(A² − x²), from conservation of energy.

**Working:** v = 9√(0.13² − 0.052²) = 1.07 m/s.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$1.17\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: That is the maximum speed, at x = 0.
- $$0.702\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Use A² − x² under a square root.
- $$0.468\ \mathrm{m/s}$$: Speed is greatest at equilibrium, not at the ends.

### Free-response practice: A mass on a spring

A block of mass 0.5 kg on a horizontal frictionless surface is attached to a spring. Its position is $$x(t) = 0.06\cos(7t)$$, with x in meters and t in seconds.

**(a)** Sketch (describe) graphs of position, velocity and acceleration versus time for one period, aligned vertically.

To earn the point: Position is a cosine starting at +A; velocity is a negative sine starting at 0. Model answer: x starts at 0.06 m; v starts at 0 and goes negative.

To earn the point: Acceleration starts at its negative maximum and is opposite in sign to position throughout. Model answer: $$a = -\omega^2 x$$

**(b)** Derive expressions for v(t) and a(t), and determine the maximum speed and maximum acceleration.

To earn the point: Correct derivatives. Model answer: $$v = -0.42\sin(7t)$$, $$a = -2.94\cos(7t)$$

To earn the point: Correct maxima. Model answer: v_max = 0.42 m/s; a_max = 2.94 m/s²

**(c)** Calculate the spring constant and the period.

To earn the point: Spring constant. Model answer: k = mω² = 24.5 N/m

To earn the point: Period. Model answer: T = 2π/ω = 0.898 s

**(d)** Calculate the total mechanical energy and describe how kinetic and potential energy vary during one period.

To earn the point: Total energy. Model answer: E = ½kA² = 0.0441 J

To earn the point: Energy exchange. Model answer: K is maximum at x = 0 and zero at x = ±A; U is the reverse; the sum is constant. Each varies twice per period.
