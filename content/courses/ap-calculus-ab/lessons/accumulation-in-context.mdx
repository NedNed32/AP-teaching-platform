Question 1 of the free-response section is almost always a rate problem: water in a tank, people in a park, cars on a road. The integral of a rate gives an amount, and the net change theorem stitches everything together.

## The template

If a quantity $$A$$ has inflow rate $$I(t)$$ and outflow rate $$O(t)$$:

$$
A(T) = A(0) + \int_0^T I(t)\,dt - \int_0^T O(t)\,dt.
$$

The amount is increasing when $$I(t) > O(t)$$, and it reaches a maximum or minimum where $$I(t) - O(t)$$ changes sign (or at an endpoint).

## Interpreting an integral

A full interpretation of $$\int_a^b R(t)\,dt$$ names:

1. the quantity (the amount, not the rate),
2. the time interval,
3. the units (rate units times time units).

For example: "$$\int_0^4 R(t)\,dt = 30$$ means 30 gallons of water flowed into the tank during the first 4 hours."

<Note title="Rate versus amount">
$$R(3)$$ is a **rate** at an instant (gallons per hour). $$\int_0^3 R(t)\,dt$$ is an **amount** (gallons). Mixing these up is the most common error in context problems.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="When is the amount largest?">
People enter a store at $$E(t) = 20 + 10\sin\left(\frac{t}{2}\right)$$ and leave at $$L(t) = 2t + 15$$ people per hour, for $$0 \leq t \leq 8$$. At what time is the number of people in the store greatest?

1. Solve $$E(t) - L(t) = 0$$ on a calculator: $$t \approx 5.161$$.
2. $$E - L$$ changes from positive to negative there, so the number of people increases, then decreases.
3. The single critical point with this sign change gives the absolute maximum; the endpoints cannot be larger.

**Answer:** at about $$t = 5.161$$ hours.
</WorkedExample>

### Free-response practice: Water in a tank

Water enters a tank at a rate modeled by $$E(t) = 11 + 5\sin\left(\frac{t}{3}\right)$$ gallons per hour and leaves the tank at a rate modeled by $$L(t) = 5\sqrt{t + 1}$$ gallons per hour, for $$0 \leq t \leq 8$$. At time $$t = 0$$ the tank contains 118 gallons of water.

**(a)** How many gallons of water enter the tank during the time interval $$0 \leq t \leq 8$$?

To earn the point: Writes a definite integral of $$E$$ over the interval. Model answer: $$\int_0^{8} E(t)\,dt$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct value. Model answer: 116.340 gallons

**(b)** Is the amount of water in the tank increasing or decreasing at time $$t = 3$$? Give a reason for your answer.

To earn the point: Concludes correctly, with a reason that compares $$E$$ and $$L$$ (or uses the sign of $$E - L$$). Model answer: $$E(3) - L(3) \approx 5.207$$, which is positive, so the amount is increasing.

**(c)** At what time $$t$$, for $$0 \leq t \leq 8$$, is the amount of water in the tank greatest? Justify your answer.

To earn the point: Sets $$E(t) - L(t) = 0$$ to find the critical points. Model answer: $$t \approx 7.228$$

To earn the point: Evaluates the amount at the critical points and the endpoints (Candidates Test). Model answer: $$W(0.000) \approx 118.000$$, $$W(7.228) \approx 148.324$$, $$W(8.000) \approx 147.673$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct time with justification. Model answer: The amount is greatest at $$t \approx 7.228$$.

**(d)** How many gallons of water are in the tank at time $$t = 8$$?

To earn the point: Uses the initial amount plus the integral of the net rate. Model answer: $$118 + \int_0^{8} (E(t) - L(t))\,dt$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct value. Model answer: 147.673 gallons
