When the region does not reach the axis of revolution, the solid has a hole through the middle. Each slice is then a washer: a disc with a smaller disc removed.

## The washer method

$$
V = \pi\int_a^b \left([R(x)]^2 - [r(x)]^2\right)dx,
$$

where

- $$R(x)$$ is the **outer** radius, from the axis to the farther boundary, and
- $$r(x)$$ is the **inner** radius, from the axis to the nearer boundary.

## About the x-axis

For a region between $$y = f(x)$$ (top) and $$y = g(x)$$ (bottom), both above the x-axis: $$R = f(x)$$, $$r = g(x)$$.

## About the y-axis

Write the boundaries as $$x$$ in terms of $$y$$ and integrate $$dy$$: $$R$$ is the boundary farther from the y-axis, $$r$$ the nearer one.

<Note title="Square first, then subtract">
The area of a washer is $$\pi R^2 - \pi r^2$$. Writing $$\pi(R - r)^2$$ is one of the most common errors in the course, and it appears as a distractor in multiple choice.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="A washer about the x-axis">
Revolve the region between $$y = x$$ and $$y = x^2$$ about the x-axis. Find the volume.

1. Intersections: $$x = 0$$ and $$x = 1$$; on $$(0, 1)$$, $$x > x^2$$.
2. $$R = x$$, $$r = x^2$$.
3. $$V = \pi\int_0^1 (x^2 - x^4)\,dx = \pi\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5}\right)$$.

**Answer:** $$\frac{2\pi}{15} \approx 0.419$$.
</WorkedExample>

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** The region enclosed by $$y = 2x$$ and $$y = x^2$$ is revolved about the x-axis. What is the volume of the resulting solid?

**A.** $$\frac{64\pi}{15}$$. $$\pi\int_0^{2} \left[(2x)^2 - (x^2)^2\right]dx$$: outer radius squared minus inner radius squared. $$V = \pi\int_0^{2} (4x^2 - x^4)\,dx = \pi\left(\frac{4x^3}{3} - \frac{x^5}{5}\right)\Big|_0^{2} = \frac{64\pi}{15}$$.

**Q.** The region enclosed by $$y = x$$ and $$y = x^2$$ is revolved about the x-axis. What is the volume of the resulting solid?

**A.** $$\frac{2\pi}{15}$$. $$\pi\int_0^{1} \left[(x)^2 - (x^2)^2\right]dx$$: outer radius squared minus inner radius squared. $$V = \pi\int_0^{1} (x^2 - x^4)\,dx = \pi\left(\frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{x^5}{5}\right)\Big|_0^{1} = \frac{2\pi}{15}$$.
