Costs connect production to prices. The shapes of the cost curves follow directly from diminishing marginal returns.

<Figure src="assets/lessons/cost-curves.svg" alt="Short-run cost curves: a U-shaped marginal cost curve crossing U-shaped average variable cost and average total cost curves at their minimum points." caption="MC crosses AVC and ATC at their minimums." />

## The formulas

| Cost | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Total cost | $$TC = FC + VC$$ |
| Average fixed cost | $$AFC = \frac{FC}{Q}$$ |
| Average variable cost | $$AVC = \frac{VC}{Q}$$ |
| Average total cost | $$ATC = \frac{TC}{Q} = AFC + AVC$$ |
| Marginal cost | $$MC = \frac{\Delta TC}{\Delta Q} = \frac{\Delta VC}{\Delta Q}$$ |

## Shapes

- **MC** first falls (increasing returns) then rises (diminishing returns).
- **AFC** always falls: the same fixed cost is spread over more units.
- **AVC** and **ATC** are U-shaped. The gap between them is AFC, which shrinks as output grows.
- **MC crosses AVC and ATC at their minimum points.** When the marginal unit costs less than the average, it pulls the average down; when it costs more, it pulls the average up.

## Shifts

- A change in **fixed cost** shifts ATC and AFC, but **not** MC or AVC.
- A change in **variable cost** (a wage, a per-unit tax) shifts MC, AVC and ATC.

<Note title="Fixed cost and the marginal decision">
Because fixed costs do not change with output, they never affect marginal cost, and so never affect how much a firm should produce in the short run.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Filling in a cost table">
A firm has fixed cost 60. Its total cost at 0, 1, 2 and 3 units is 60, 100, 130 and 170. Find MC for each unit and ATC at 3 units.

1. MC: $$100 - 60 = 40$$, $$130 - 100 = 30$$, $$170 - 130 = 40$$.
2. ATC at 3 units: $$\frac{170}{3} \approx 56.67$$.

**Answer:** MC of 40, 30, 40; ATC at 3 units is about 56.67.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows a firm's total cost at each level of output.

*Total cost*

| Output | Total cost |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 70 |
| 1 | 94 |
| 2 | 113 |
| 3 | 140 |
| 4 | 173 |
| 5 | 209 |
| 6 | 250 |

What is the marginal cost of producing 2 units?

**Answer:** 19

**Why:** TC(2) − TC(1).

**Working:** Fixed cost is the cost at zero output, 70. At 2 units: MC = 19, ATC = 56.50, AVC = 21.50, AFC = 35.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 56.50: This is the average total cost.
- 21.50: This is the average variable cost.
- 35: This is the average fixed cost.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** A one-time license fee is imposed on the firm. How are the firm's short-run cost curves affected?

**A.** ATC and AFC shift up; MC and AVC do not change.. Fixed costs affect only average fixed and total costs; variable costs affect marginal and average variable costs too. A change in fixed cost shifts AFC and ATC but leaves MC and AVC unchanged. A change in a per-unit (variable) cost shifts MC, AVC and ATC.

**Q.** The price of raw materials used in each unit falls. How are the firm's short-run cost curves affected?

**A.** MC, AVC and ATC all shift down.. Fixed costs affect only average fixed and total costs; variable costs affect marginal and average variable costs too. A change in fixed cost shifts AFC and ATC but leaves MC and AVC unchanged. A change in a per-unit (variable) cost shifts MC, AVC and ATC.

**Q.** The rent on a firm's building rises. How are the firm's short-run cost curves affected?

**A.** ATC and AFC shift up; MC and AVC do not change.. Fixed costs affect only average fixed and total costs; variable costs affect marginal and average variable costs too. A change in fixed cost shifts AFC and ATC but leaves MC and AVC unchanged. A change in a per-unit (variable) cost shifts MC, AVC and ATC.

**Q.** The wage paid to every worker rises. How are the firm's short-run cost curves affected?

**A.** MC, AVC and ATC all shift up.. Fixed costs affect only average fixed and total costs; variable costs affect marginal and average variable costs too. A change in fixed cost shifts AFC and ATC but leaves MC and AVC unchanged. A change in a per-unit (variable) cost shifts MC, AVC and ATC.
