Supply describes how much of a good producers are willing and able to sell at each price.

## The law of supply

A higher price makes production more profitable and covers the rising cost of producing extra units, so producers offer more. The supply curve slopes **upward**.

## Supply shifters

| Determinant | Increase in supply (shift right) when... |
| --- | --- |
| Input (resource) prices | input prices fall |
| Technology | production methods improve |
| Taxes and subsidies | per-unit taxes fall or subsidies rise |
| Prices of other goods the firm could make | the price of an alternative product falls |
| Expectations | producers expect lower future prices (sell more now) |
| Number of sellers | more firms enter the market |

Regulation that raises costs, and natural disasters that destroy capacity, decrease supply.

<Note title="Shift right means more, not higher">
An increase in supply shifts the curve **right** (more offered at each price). On a graph that can look like a downward shift, which confuses many students. Think "right equals more".
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Two shifters at once">
A new machine cuts the cost of making shoes, and the wage of shoemakers rises. Can you tell what happens to the supply of shoes?

1. Better technology increases supply.
2. Higher wages (an input price) decrease supply.
3. Without knowing which effect is larger, the net direction is uncertain.

**Answer:** the change in supply is indeterminate.
</WorkedExample>

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** In the market for restaurant meals, consumer incomes rise and restaurant meals are a normal good. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of restaurant meals?

**A.** Price will increase; quantity will increase. Higher income raises demand for a normal good. Higher income raises demand for a normal good: demand increases. A demand shift moves price and quantity in the same direction.

**Q.** In the market for smartphones, many new firms enter the market. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of smartphones?

**A.** Price will decrease; quantity will increase. More sellers increase supply. More sellers increase supply: supply increases. A supply shift moves price and quantity in opposite directions.
