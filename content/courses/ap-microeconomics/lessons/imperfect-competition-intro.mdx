Most real markets are not perfectly competitive. Firms differentiate their products, a few large firms dominate, or a single firm controls the market.

## The four structures

| Structure | Firms | Product | Entry | Long-run profit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perfect competition | very many | identical | free | zero |
| Monopolistic competition | many | differentiated | easy | zero |
| Oligopoly | few | identical or differentiated | difficult | possible |
| Monopoly | one | unique | blocked | possible |

## Downward-sloping demand and MR

Any firm with market power faces a downward-sloping demand curve. To sell an extra unit, it must cut the price on **every** unit it sells, so

$$
MR < P.
$$

For a straight-line demand curve $$P = a - bQ$$, the MR curve is $$MR = a - 2bQ$$: same intercept, twice as steep.

<Note title="Same rule, different outcome">
All firms maximize profit where MR = MC. The difference is that with market power, price (read from the demand curve) is above MR, and therefore above MC.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Marginal revenue">
A firm can sell 4 units at a price of 10 or 5 units at a price of 9. What is the marginal revenue of the 5th unit?

1. TR at 4 units: 40. TR at 5 units: 45.
2. MR = 45 − 40 = 5.

**Answer:** MR is 5, well below the price of 9, because the price cut applies to all five units.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A firm can sell 7 units at a price of 25 each, or 8 units at a price of 23 each. What is the marginal revenue of unit 8?

**Answer:** 9

**Why:** 184 − 175.

**Working:** TR(7) = 175; TR(8) = 184. MR = 9.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 23: Price exceeds marginal revenue because the price cut applies to all units.
- 184: This is total revenue.
- 2: This is the price cut, not the change in revenue.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which market structure is described? In long-run equilibrium, price equals average total cost but exceeds marginal cost, and firms have excess capacity.

**A.** Monopolistic competition. This matches the structure's long-run conditions. Perfect competition: P = MC = min ATC. Monopolistic competition: P = ATC > MC, excess capacity. Oligopoly: few interdependent firms. Monopoly: one firm, barriers, possible long-run profit.

**Q.** Which market structure is described? A few interdependent firms consider rivals' reactions when setting prices.

**A.** Oligopoly. This matches the structure's long-run conditions. Perfect competition: P = MC = min ATC. Monopolistic competition: P = ATC > MC, excess capacity. Oligopoly: few interdependent firms. Monopoly: one firm, barriers, possible long-run profit.

**Q.** Which market structure is described? In long-run equilibrium, price equals marginal cost and minimum average total cost.

**A.** Perfect competition. This matches the structure's long-run conditions. Perfect competition: P = MC = min ATC. Monopolistic competition: P = ATC > MC, excess capacity. Oligopoly: few interdependent firms. Monopoly: one firm, barriers, possible long-run profit.
