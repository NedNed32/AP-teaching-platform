Wages are prices determined by supply and demand in labor markets. Knowing what shifts each curve lets you predict wages and employment.

## Shifts in labor demand

| Change | Labor demand | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product price rises | increases | MRP = MP × P rises |
| Productivity rises (better training, more capital) | increases | MP rises |
| Price of a **substitute** factor falls (cheaper robots) | decreases | firms switch to the substitute |
| Price of a **complement** factor falls (cheaper computers for programmers) | increases | workers become more useful |

## Shifts in labor supply

- More workers (immigration, population growth): supply increases.
- Higher wages in other occupations: supply to this one decreases.
- Licensing or long training requirements: supply decreases.
- Changing preferences for work versus leisure.

<Note title="Substitute and complement factors">
A cheaper substitute factor can reduce labor demand through substitution but increase it if lower costs expand output a lot. The AP exam usually expects the substitution effect for substitutes and the increase for complements.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="A rise in product demand">
Demand for electric cars soars, raising their price. What happens to the wage and employment of battery engineers?

1. Higher car prices raise the MRP of engineers who help make them.
2. Labor demand increases.
3. With an upward-sloping supply of engineers, both the wage and employment rise.

**Answer:** the wage and employment of battery engineers both increase.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** In a competitive labor market, a new licensing requirement makes it harder to enter the occupation. Which of the following is most likely?

**A.** Labor supply decreases; the wage rises and employment falls.. Identify whether firms' MRP (demand) or workers' availability (supply) changed. Labor demand depends on MRP (product price and productivity). Labor supply depends on the number of available workers and entry requirements.

**Q.** In a competitive labor market, the price of the product that the workers make rises. Which of the following is most likely?
