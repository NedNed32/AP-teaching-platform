$$\Delta H \approx \sum D_{\text{broken}} - \sum D_{\text{formed}}$$

Breaking bonds costs energy (positive); forming bonds releases energy. If the bonds formed are stronger than those broken, the reaction is exothermic.

<Note title="Broken minus formed">
This is the reverse of the formation-enthalpy formula (products minus reactants). Do not mix them up.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Estimate">
Estimate ΔH for H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl (H–H 436, Cl–Cl 242, H–Cl 431 kJ/mol).

1. Broken: 436 + 242 = 678.
2. Formed: 2(431) = 862.

**Answer:** 678 − 862 = −184 kJ.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** Use the bond enthalpies to estimate ΔH for $$\ce{H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl}$$.

**A.** $$-184\ \mathrm{kJ}$$. ΔH ≈ Σ(bonds broken) − Σ(bonds formed). Broken 678 − formed 862 = -184 kJ.
