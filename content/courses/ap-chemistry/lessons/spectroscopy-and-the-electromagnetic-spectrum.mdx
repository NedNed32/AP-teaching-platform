| Region | Effect on molecules |
| --- | --- |
| microwave | rotations |
| infrared | vibrations |
| visible / ultraviolet | electronic transitions |

Higher frequency means higher energy and shorter wavelength.

<Note title="Identify the transition">
Questions often describe the absorbed radiation; match it to rotation, vibration or electronic change.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Match">
A molecule absorbs infrared radiation. What happens?

1. IR matches vibrational energy levels.

**Answer:** its bonds vibrate more (vibrational transition).
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

What is the energy of a photon with a wavelength of 609 nm?

**Answer:** $$3.26 \times 10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$$

**Why:** E = hc/λ, with λ in meters.

**Working:** E = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴)(3.00 × 10⁸)/(609 × 10⁻⁹) = 3.26 \times 10^\{-19\} J.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$3.26 \times 10^{-28}\ \mathrm{J}$$: Convert nm to m.
- $$4.04 \times 10^{-40}\ \mathrm{J}$$: E = hc/λ.
- $$1.35 \times 10^{-48}\ \mathrm{J}$$: E = hc/λ.
