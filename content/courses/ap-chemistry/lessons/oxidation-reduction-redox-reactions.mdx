**Oxidation number rules**: elements = 0; monatomic ions = charge; O usually −2; H usually +1; the sum equals the overall charge.

"OIL RIG": Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons).

Balance redox reactions by splitting into half-reactions, balancing atoms and charge (with electrons), then equalizing electrons.

<Note title="Agents">
The oxidizing agent is reduced; the reducing agent is oxidized.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Balance">
Balance Al + Cu²⁺ → Al³⁺ + Cu.

1. Al → Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ (×2); Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (×3).

**Answer:** $$\ce{2Al + 3Cu^{2+} -> 2Al^{3+} + 3Cu}$$.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** What is the oxidation number of Mn in $$\ce{MnO4^-}$$?

**A.** +7. The oxidation numbers must sum to the species' overall charge (O usually −2, H usually +1). Mn in MnO4^-: +7.
