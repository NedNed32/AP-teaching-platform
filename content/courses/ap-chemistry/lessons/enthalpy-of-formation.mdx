$$\Delta H^\circ_{rxn} = \sum n\,\Delta H^\circ_f(\text{products}) - \sum n\,\Delta H^\circ_f(\text{reactants})$$

Elements in their standard states have $$\Delta H^\circ_f = 0$$.

<Note title="Multiply by coefficients">
Each ΔH°f is per mole; multiply by the coefficient in the balanced equation.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Calculate">
Find ΔH° for $$\ce{CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O(l)}$$ (ΔH°f: CH₄ −75, CO₂ −394, H₂O(l) −286 kJ/mol).

1. Products: −394 + 2(−286) = −966.
2. Reactants: −75 + 0 = −75.

**Answer:** −966 − (−75) = −891 kJ.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** Calculate ΔH° for $$\ce{2CO + O2 -> 2CO2}$$. (ΔH°f of O₂ is 0.)

**A.** $$-566\ \mathrm{kJ}$$. Σ n ΔH°f(products) − Σ n ΔH°f(reactants). Products -787 − reactants -221 = -566 kJ.
