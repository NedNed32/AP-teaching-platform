A molecule is **polar** if it has polar bonds arranged asymmetrically. Single bonds are σ bonds; double bonds have one σ and one π bond.

<Note title="Lone pairs squeeze angles">
Lone pairs repel more than bonding pairs, so angles shrink below the ideal values.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Predict">
Predict the shape, polarity and hybridization of PCl₃.

1. P has three bonds and one lone pair: four domains.
2. Trigonal pyramidal; asymmetric so polar.
3. sp³.

**Answer:** trigonal pyramidal, polar, sp³.
</WorkedExample>
