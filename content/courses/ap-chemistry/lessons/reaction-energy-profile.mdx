An energy profile plots potential energy against reaction progress.

- **Eₐ** (forward): transition state − reactants.
- **ΔH**: products − reactants (negative for exothermic).
- Reverse Eₐ = forward Eₐ − ΔH.

<Note title="Exothermic profile">
Products lie below reactants; the hill still has to be climbed first.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Read a profile">
Reactants are at 50 kJ, the transition state at 130 kJ, and products at 20 kJ. Find Eₐ and ΔH.

1. $$E_a = 130 - 50 = 80$$ kJ.
2. $$\Delta H = 20 - 50 = -30$$ kJ.

**Answer:** Eₐ = 80 kJ; ΔH = −30 kJ (exothermic).
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

On a reaction energy profile, the reactants are at 71 kJ/mol, the transition state at 133 kJ/mol and the products at 68 kJ/mol. What is the activation energy of the reverse reaction?

**Answer:** $$65\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$

**Why:** Reverse Ea = transition state − products.

**Working:** Forward Ea = 62, ΔH = -3, reverse Ea = 65 kJ/mol.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$62\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Measure from the correct starting point.
- $$-3\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Check the sign and direction.
- $$133\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Subtract the starting energy.

### Worked exam question 2

On a reaction energy profile, the reactants are at 68 kJ/mol, the transition state at 166 kJ/mol and the products at 3 kJ/mol. What is ΔH for the reaction?

**Answer:** $$-65\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$

**Why:** ΔH = products − reactants.

**Working:** Forward Ea = 98, ΔH = -65, reverse Ea = 163 kJ/mol.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$98\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Measure from the correct starting point.
- $$65\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Check the sign and direction.
- $$166\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Subtract the starting energy.

### Worked exam question 3

**Why:** Ea = transition state − reactants.

**Working:** Forward Ea = 136, ΔH = -79, reverse Ea = 215 kJ/mol.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$215\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Measure from the correct starting point.
- $$-79\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Check the sign and direction.
- $$219\ \mathrm{kJ/mol}$$: Subtract the starting energy.
