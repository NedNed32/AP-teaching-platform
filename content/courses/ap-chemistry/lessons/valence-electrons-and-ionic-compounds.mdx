Main-group elements tend to gain or lose electrons to reach a noble-gas configuration:

| Group | Ion |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | +1 |
| 2 | +2 |
| 13 | +3 |
| 15 | −3 |
| 16 | −2 |
| 17 | −1 |

Ionic formulas balance total charge: $$\ce{Ca^{2+}}$$ and $$\ce{Cl^-}$$ give $$\ce{CaCl2}$$.

Elements in the same group form compounds with similar formulas ($$\ce{NaCl}$$, $$\ce{KCl}$$).

<Note title="Predicting with the periodic table">
If you know one element's compound formula, others in the same group usually follow the same pattern.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Write a formula">
Write the formula of the compound formed by magnesium and nitrogen.

1. $$\ce{Mg^{2+}}$$ and $$\ce{N^{3-}}$$.
2. 3(+2) = 6 = 2(−3) in magnitude.

**Answer:** $$\ce{Mg3N2}$$.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which formula is most likely for the compound of element X (Group 2) and element Y (Group 16)?

**A.** $$\ce{XY}$$. X²⁺ and Y²⁻ combine 1 : 1.

**Q.** Why is the radius of F⁻ larger than that of F?

**A.** The added electron increases electron–electron repulsion with the same nuclear charge. Anions are larger than their atoms.

**Q.** Which element has the highest electronegativity?

**A.** Fluorine. Electronegativity increases up and to the right.

**Q.** Which best explains why the atomic radius of Cl is smaller than that of Na?

**A.** Cl has more protons with similar shielding, so its valence electrons are pulled closer. Greater effective nuclear charge across a period.

**Q.** Which has a larger first ionization energy, Mg or Ba, and why?

**A.** Mg, because its valence electrons are closer to the nucleus. Down a group, electrons are farther away and more shielded.
