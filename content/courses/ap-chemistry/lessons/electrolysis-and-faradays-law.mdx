$$q = It \qquad n_{e^-} = \frac{q}{F} \qquad F = 96{,}485\ \text{C/mol}$$

Then use the half-reaction's electron ratio to find moles (and mass) of product.

<Note title="Chain of conversions">
amps × seconds → coulombs → mol e⁻ → mol metal → grams.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Electroplating">
How many grams of Cu (63.55 g/mol) are deposited by 3.00 A for 30.0 min?

1. $$q = 3.00 \times 1800 = 5400$$ C; 0.0560 mol e⁻.
2. 0.0280 mol Cu.

**Answer:** 1.78 g.
</WorkedExample>
