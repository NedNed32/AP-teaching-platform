$$\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K \qquad K = e^{-\Delta G^\circ/RT}$$

| ΔG° | K |
| --- | --- |
| negative | greater than 1 (products favored) |
| 0 | 1 |
| positive | less than 1 |

Use R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) with ΔG° in J/mol.

<Note title="Sign shortcut">
Negative ΔG° always means K greater than 1.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Find K">
ΔG° = −11.4 kJ/mol at 298 K. Find K.

1. $$\ln K = 11400/(8.314 \times 298) = 4.60$$.

**Answer:** K ≈ 99.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** At 298 K, ΔG° for a reaction is 14 kJ/mol. What is K?

**A.** $$3.52 \times 10^{-3}\ \mathrm{}$$. K = e^(−ΔG°/RT), with ΔG° in J/mol and R = 8.314. ln K = -5.65; K = 3.52 \times 10^\{-3\}. Positive ΔG°: K less than 1.
