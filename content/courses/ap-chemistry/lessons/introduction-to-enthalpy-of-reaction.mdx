ΔH refers to the reaction **as written**, in moles. Multiply by the number of moles that react:

$$q = n \times \Delta H_{rxn}$$

Reversing a reaction changes the sign; multiplying coefficients multiplies ΔH.

<Note title="Per mole of what?">
Use the mole ratio from the equation: ΔH for 2H₂ + O₂ applies to 2 mol H₂.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Heat released">
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, ΔH = −572 kJ. How much heat is released when 8.0 g of H₂ burns?

1. 8.0 g = 4.0 mol H₂ = twice the equation.

**Answer:** 1144 kJ released.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Why does temperature remain constant while ice melts?

**A.** Added energy overcomes intermolecular forces rather than increasing kinetic energy. Phase changes occur at constant temperature.

**Q.** If ΔH = −286 kJ for H₂ + ½O₂ → H₂O(l), what is ΔH for 2H₂O(l) → 2H₂ + O₂?

**A.** +572 kJ. Reverse (change sign) and double.

**Q.** Why is the enthalpy of vaporization of water much larger than its enthalpy of fusion?

**A.** Vaporization separates molecules completely, overcoming all intermolecular forces. Melting only loosens them.

**Q.** When a salt dissolves in water, the temperature of the solution decreases. Which is true?

**A.** The process is endothermic; energy flows from the water into the dissolving process. The surroundings lose energy.
