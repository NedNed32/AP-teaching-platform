| x | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P(X = x) | 0.10 | 0.35 | 0.40 | 0.15 |

- Each probability is between 0 and 1; they add to 1.
- Probabilities of ranges come from adding: $$P(X \ge 2) = 0.40 + 0.15 = 0.55$$.

**Continuous** random variables have probabilities given by areas under a density curve.

<Note title="Missing value">
If one probability is missing, find it by subtracting the others from 1.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Missing probability">
P(X = 0) = 0.2, P(X = 1) = 0.5, P(X = 2) = ?, and X takes only 0, 1, 2. Find P(X = 2).

1. 1 − 0.2 − 0.5.

**Answer:** 0.3.
</WorkedExample>
