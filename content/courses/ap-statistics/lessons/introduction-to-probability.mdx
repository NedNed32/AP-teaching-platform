- Probabilities are between 0 and 1, and all outcomes in the sample space add to 1.
- With equally likely outcomes: $$P(A) = \frac{\text{outcomes in } A}{\text{total outcomes}}$$
- Complement rule: $$P(A^C) = 1 - P(A)$$

The probability of an event is its long-run relative frequency over many repetitions.

<Note title="At least one">
For 'at least one' questions, use the complement: P(at least one) = 1 − P(none).
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Complement">
The probability a part is defective is 0.04, independently. What is the probability that at least one of 5 parts is defective?

1. $$P(\text{none}) = 0.96^5 = 0.815$$.
2. $$1 - 0.815$$.

**Answer:** about 0.185.
</WorkedExample>

### Worked exam question 1

Each of 6 independent devices fails with probability 0.02. What is the probability that at least one fails?

**Answer:** 0.1142

**Why:** 1 − P(none fail) = 1 − (1 − p)^n.

**Working:** 1 − 0.98^6 = 0.1142.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 0.12: Probabilities of overlapping events cannot simply be added.
- 0: That is the probability all fail.
- 0.8858: That is the probability none fail.
