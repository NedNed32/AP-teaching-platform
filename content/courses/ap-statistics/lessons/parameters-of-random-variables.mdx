$$\mu_X = \sum x\,P(x) \qquad \sigma_X = \sqrt{\sum (x - \mu_X)^2 P(x)}$$

## Transformations

For $$Y = a + bX$$: $$\mu_Y = a + b\mu_X$$ and $$\sigma_Y = |b|\sigma_X$$.

## Combining independent random variables

$$\mu_{X \pm Y} = \mu_X \pm \mu_Y \qquad \sigma_{X \pm Y} = \sqrt{\sigma_X^2 + \sigma_Y^2}$$

Variances add even for differences; standard deviations do not add.

<Note title="Interpret the mean">
The expected value is the long-run average of many repetitions, not necessarily a value that can occur.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Expected value">
A game pays $10 with probability 0.1, $2 with probability 0.3, and $0 otherwise. Find the expected payout.

1. $$10(0.1) + 2(0.3) + 0 = 1.6$$.

**Answer:** $1.60 per play.
</WorkedExample>

### Worked exam question 1

X and Y are independent with μX = 40, σX = 6, μY = 35, σY = 5. What are the mean and standard deviation of X − Y?

**Answer:** Mean 5, SD 7.81

**Why:** Means add or subtract; variances add.

**Working:** μ = 5; σ = √(6² + 5²) = 7.81.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Mean 5, SD 1: Standard deviations do not add; variances do.
- Mean 75, SD 7.81: Check the sign of the mean.
- Mean 5, SD 61: That is the variance; take the square root.

### Worked exam question 2

X and Y are independent with μX = 40, σX = 4, μY = 12, σY = 5. What are the mean and standard deviation of X + Y?

**Answer:** Mean 52, SD 6.4

**Why:** Means add or subtract; variances add.

**Working:** μ = 52; σ = √(4² + 5²) = 6.4.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Mean 52, SD 9: Standard deviations do not add; variances do.
- Mean 28, SD 6.4: Check the sign of the mean.
- Mean 52, SD 41: That is the variance; take the square root.
