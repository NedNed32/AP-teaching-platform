$$\chi^2 = \sum \frac{(O - E)^2}{E} \qquad df = (r - 1)(c - 1)$$

The p-value is the area to the right of χ² under the chi-square distribution with those degrees of freedom. Chi-square tests are always right-tailed.

After rejecting H₀, look at the largest components to describe **how** the distributions differ.

<Note title="Counts, not percents">
Chi-square calculations must use counts. Converting to percentages changes the statistic.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Compute">
A 2 × 2 table has observed counts 30, 20 / 20, 30 and every expected count is 25. Find χ², df and the conclusion at α = 0.05.

1. Each component: $$5^2/25 = 1$$; χ² = 4.
2. df = 1; p-value ≈ 0.046.

**Answer:** reject H₀: there is convincing evidence of an association.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Observed counts*

| Group | Option A | Option B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | 10 | 42 |
| Group 2 | 24 | 12 |

Assuming the null hypothesis of a chi-square test is true, what is the expected count for Group 1, Option A?

**Answer:** 20.09

**Why:** Expected = (row total × column total) / table total.

**Working:** 52 × 34 / 88 = 20.09.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 10: That is the observed count.
- 22: Expected counts depend on the row and column totals.
- 10.05: Divide by the table total once.

### Worked exam question 2

*Observed counts*

| Group | Option A | Option B | Option C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Group 1 | 52 | 32 | 28 |
| Group 2 | 35 | 37 | 26 |

For a chi-square test of homogeneity on these counts, what are the test statistic and degrees of freedom?

**Answer:** χ² = 2.84, df = 2, p-value = 0.242

**Why:** χ² = Σ(O − E)²/E with df = (rows − 1)(columns − 1).

**Working:** χ² = 2.84 with df = 2; p-value = 0.242.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- χ² = 2.84, df = 5, p-value = 0.725: For a two-way table, df = (r − 1)(c − 1).
- χ² = 0.0135, df = 2, p-value = 0.9933: Do not divide by the total.
- χ² = 1.68, df = 2, p-value = 0.4307: Do not take a square root.
