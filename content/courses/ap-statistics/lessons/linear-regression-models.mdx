$$\hat y = a + bx$$

- **Slope b**: "For each 1-unit increase in x, the predicted y increases (or decreases) by b units."
- **Intercept a**: the predicted y when x = 0 (may not make sense in context).
- Use **ŷ** (predicted), not y.

Avoid **extrapolation**: predictions far outside the observed x values are unreliable.

<Note title="Predicted, not actual">
Interpretations should say 'predicted' because individual values vary around the line.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Predict">
ŷ = 12 + 4.5x predicts weekly earnings (in tens of dollars) from hours worked. Predict for x = 10.

1. $$12 + 4.5(10) = 57$$.

**Answer:** 57 (that is, $570) predicted.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The regression line is ŷ = 33 − 3x. An observation has x = 12 and y = 2.6. What is its residual, and what does it mean?

**Answer:** 5.6; the actual value is above the predicted value

**Why:** Residual = y − ŷ.

**Working:** ŷ = -3; residual = 2.6 − -3 = 5.6.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- -5.6; the actual value is below the predicted value: Residual = actual − predicted, not predicted − actual.
- -3; this is the residual: That is the predicted value ŷ.
- 5.6; the actual value is below the predicted value: A positive residual means the point is above the line.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** A point with an x value far from the others noticeably changes the slope when removed. What is it?

**A.** An influential point. Removing it changes the regression line substantially.

**Q.** A residual plot shows a clear curved pattern. What does this indicate?

**A.** A linear model is not appropriate for the data. Curvature in residuals means the relationship is not linear.

**Q.** A regression of weight (kg) on height (cm) for children aged 5–10 is used to predict the weight of an adult who is 180 cm tall. What is the main problem?

**A.** Extrapolation beyond the range of the data. The pattern may not hold for adults.

**Q.** Which statement about the correlation r is true?

**A.** r does not change if the units of x or y change. r has no units.

**Q.** The slope of a regression line predicting exam score from hours studied is 3.5. Which interpretation is correct?

**A.** For each additional hour studied, the predicted exam score increases by about 3.5 points. Slope is the predicted change in y per unit of x.
