$$z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$$

For a normal distribution $$N(\mu, \sigma)$$:

- **Probability from a value**: standardize, then find the area (normalcdf).
- **Value from a percentile**: find the z with that area (invNorm), then $$x = \mu + z\sigma$$.

Empirical rule: 68–95–99.7.

Show your work: name the distribution, the boundary, the area of interest, and the answer in context.

<Note title="Is it normal?">
Only use normal calculations when the distribution is approximately normal, or when a sampling distribution is justified to be approximately normal.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Percentile">
Test scores are N(500, 100). What score is at the 90th percentile?

1. invNorm(0.90) gives z ≈ 1.282.
2. $$500 + 1.282(100)$$.

**Answer:** about 628.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A variable is approximately normal with mean 118 and standard deviation 5. What proportion of values are greater than 116?

**Answer:** 0.6554

**Why:** z = (116 − 118)/5 = -0.4.

**Working:** z = -0.4; area to the right = 0.6554.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 0.3446: That is the other side of the boundary.
- 0.04: Use the normal distribution's area, not the z-score.
- 0.4207: Divide by σ when standardizing.

### Worked exam question 2

A variable is approximately normal with mean 83 and standard deviation 13. What proportion of values are less than 107.7?

**Answer:** 0.9713

**Why:** z = (107.7 − 83)/13 = 1.9.

**Working:** z = 1.9; area to the left = 0.9713.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 0.0287: That is the other side of the boundary.
- 0.19: Use the normal distribution's area, not the z-score.
- 0.8289: Divide by σ when standardizing.

### Worked exam question 3

A variable is approximately normal with mean 67 and standard deviation 11. What value is at the 90th percentile?

**Answer:** 81.1

**Why:** z = invNorm(0.9) ≈ 1.282; x = μ + zσ.

**Working:** x = 67 + 1.282(11) = 81.1.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 52.9: Check the sign of z.
- 76.9: Use the z-score for the percentile, not the percentile itself.
- 222.07: Multiply z by σ, not σ².
