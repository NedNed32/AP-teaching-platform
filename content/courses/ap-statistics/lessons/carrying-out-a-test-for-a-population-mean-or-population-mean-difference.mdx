$$t = \frac{\bar x - \mu_0}{s/\sqrt n} \qquad df = n - 1$$

Find the p-value from the t distribution (tcdf), compare with α, and conclude in context.

<Note title="Same four steps">
State, plan, do, conclude: the structure is the same for every significance test.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Test">
n = 25, x̄ = 365.2 g, s = 6 g. Test H₀: μ = 368 vs Hₐ: μ < 368 at α = 0.05.

1. $$t = (365.2 - 368)/(6/5) = -2.33$$, df = 24.
2. p-value ≈ 0.014.

**Answer:** reject H₀: convincing evidence that the true mean fill is less than 368 g.
</WorkedExample>

### Worked exam question 1

A random sample of 39 gives x̄ = 118.2 and s = 11. Test H₀: μ = 120 versus Hₐ: μ &lt; 120. What are the test statistic and p-value?

**Answer:** t = -1.02, df = 38, p-value = 0.1566

**Why:** t = (x̄ − μ₀)/(s/√n) with df = n − 1.

**Working:** t = (118.2 − 120)/(11/√39) = -1.02; p-value = 0.1566.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- t = -1.02, df = 38, p-value = 0.1534: Use the t distribution, not the normal.
- t = -0.16, df = 38, p-value = 0.4354: Divide by the standard error s/√n.
- t = -1.02, df = 38, p-value = 0.3133: The alternative is one-sided.
