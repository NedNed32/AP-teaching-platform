$$(\hat p_1 - \hat p_2) \pm z^*\sqrt{\frac{\hat p_1(1-\hat p_1)}{n_1} + \frac{\hat p_2(1-\hat p_2)}{n_2}}$$

Follow the four steps: state, plan (conditions), do, conclude.

<Note title="Define the difference">
Write it out: p₁ − p₂, where p₁ is the proportion of … and p₂ is the proportion of …
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Construct">
In random samples, 120 of 300 city residents and 90 of 300 suburban residents use public transit weekly. Find a 95% interval for p_city − p_suburb.

1. $$\hat p_1 = 0.40$$, $$\hat p_2 = 0.30$$; SE = $$\sqrt{0.24/300 + 0.21/300} = 0.0387$$.
2. $$0.10 \pm 1.96(0.0387)$$.

**Answer:** (0.024, 0.176).
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

The practice exams do not ask about this topic on its own, but it supports these questions from the same unit. Work through them to see how the ideas are used.

### Worked exam question 1

In a random sample of 210 adults in a city, 82 use public transit weekly. Which is a 95% confidence interval for the proportion of all adults in a city who use public transit weekly?

**Answer:** (0.324, 0.456)

**Why:** p̂ ± z*√(p̂(1 − p̂)/n) with z* = 1.96.

**Working:** p̂ = 0.39, SE = 0.0337, margin = 0.066.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- (0.139, 0.642): Divide by n inside the square root.
- (-0.076, 0.857): Use the standard error, √(p̂(1 − p̂)/n).
- (0.357, 0.424): Multiply the standard error by z*.

### Worked exam question 5

To test H₀: p = 0.3 versus Hₐ: p > 0.3, a random sample of 150 gives p̂ = 0.38. What are the test statistic and p-value?

**Answer:** z = 2.14, p-value = 0.0163

**Why:** z = (p̂ − p₀)/√(p₀(1 − p₀)/n); one-sided p-value.

**Working:** SD = √(0.3·0.7/150) = 0.0374; z = 2.14; p-value = 0.0163.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- z = 2.02, p-value = 0.0218: The test's standard deviation uses p₀, not p̂.
- z = 2.14, p-value = 0.0325: The alternative is one-sided.
- z = 2.14, p-value = 0.9837: Find the area in the direction of Hₐ.
