$$(\bar x_1 - \bar x_2) \pm t^*\sqrt{\frac{s_1^2}{n_1} + \frac{s_2^2}{n_2}}$$

Degrees of freedom come from technology (the Welch approximation) or conservatively $$\min(n_1 - 1, n_2 - 1)$$.

Follow the four steps and interpret in context with units.

<Note title="Do not pool">
AP Statistics uses unpooled standard errors for means.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Construct">
Group 1: n = 30, x̄ = 78, s = 8. Group 2: n = 35, x̄ = 72, s = 10. Find SE.

1. $$\sqrt{64/30 + 100/35} = \sqrt{2.133 + 2.857}$$.

**Answer:** SE ≈ 2.23; the interval is 6 ± t*(2.23).
</WorkedExample>

### Worked exam question 1

Two independent random samples have n₁ = 18, s₁ = 12 and n₂ = 24, s₂ = 10. What is the standard error of x̄₁ − x̄₂?

**Answer:** 3.488

**Why:** √(s₁²/n₁ + s₂²/n₂).

**Working:** √(12²/18 + 10²/24) = 3.488.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 1.958: Variances add, even for a difference.
- 4.87: Add variances, then take the square root; standard errors do not add.
- 3.395: Each sample contributes s²/n.
