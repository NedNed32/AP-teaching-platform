$$z = \frac{(\hat p_1 - \hat p_2) - 0}{\sqrt{\hat p_c(1 - \hat p_c)\left(\dfrac{1}{n_1} + \dfrac{1}{n_2}\right)}}$$

Find the p-value from the standard normal distribution in the direction of Hₐ, compare with α, and conclude in context. With random assignment, a significant result supports a **causal** conclusion.

<Note title="Statistical versus practical significance">
With large samples, tiny differences can be statistically significant but unimportant in practice.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Test">
Using p̂₁ = 0.30, p̂₂ = 0.20, n₁ = n₂ = 150 (p̂c = 0.25), test Hₐ: p₁ − p₂ > 0.

1. SE = $$\sqrt{0.25(0.75)(2/150)} = 0.05$$.
2. $$z = 0.10/0.05 = 2.0$$; p-value ≈ 0.023.

**Answer:** reject H₀ at α = 0.05: convincing evidence that p₁ is greater than p₂.
</WorkedExample>

### Worked exam question 1

In a randomized experiment, 80 of 210 subjects improved with treatment 1 and 128 of 250 improved with treatment 2. For a test of H₀: p₁ − p₂ = 0, what is the test statistic?

**Answer:** -2.81

**Why:** Pooled p̂c = 0.4522; z = (p̂₁ − p̂₂)/√(p̂c(1 − p̂c)(1/n₁ + 1/n₂)).

**Working:** p̂₁ = 0.381, p̂₂ = 0.512, SE = 0.0466, z = -2.81.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- -2.84: The test uses the pooled proportion, because H₀ assumes p₁ = p₂.
- -5.65: Use (1/n₁ + 1/n₂).
- -0.13: Divide the difference by its standard error.
