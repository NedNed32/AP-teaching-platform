$$\bar x = \frac{\sum x_i}{n} \qquad s = \sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - \bar x)^2}{n - 1}}$$

- **Median**: the middle value of the ordered data.
- **Quartiles** Q1 and Q3: medians of the lower and upper halves; **IQR = Q3 − Q1**.
- **Outliers** (1.5 × IQR rule): below Q1 − 1.5·IQR or above Q3 + 1.5·IQR.

In a skewed distribution the mean is pulled toward the tail: skewed right means the mean is greater than the median.

**Interpret** the standard deviation: "the typical distance of the values from the mean is about s units."

<Note title="Choose the right summary">
For skewed data or data with outliers, report the median and IQR. For roughly symmetric data, the mean and standard deviation are appropriate.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Fences">
Data: Q1 = 14, Q3 = 26. Find the outlier fences.

1. IQR = 12; 1.5 × 12 = 18.
2. Lower: 14 − 18 = −4. Upper: 26 + 18 = 44.

**Answer:** values below −4 or above 44 are outliers.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The data are: 10, 15, 15, 15, 15, 18, 19, 24, 37, 77. Using the 1.5 × IQR rule, which statement is correct?

**Answer:** Q1 = 15, Q3 = 24; values above 37.5 are outliers, so 77 is an outlier

**Why:** IQR = 9; upper fence = Q3 + 1.5·IQR = 37.5.

**Working:** Five-number summary: 10, 15, 16.5, 24, 77. Upper fence 24 + 1.5(9) = 37.5.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Q1 = 15, Q3 = 24; values above 33 are outliers, so 77 is an outlier: The fence uses 1.5 × IQR, not 1 × IQR.
- Q1 = 15, Q3 = 24; values above 30 are outliers: Add 1.5·IQR to Q3, not to the median.
- There are no outliers because every value is within 2 standard deviations of the mean: The 1.5 × IQR rule is the method asked for.

### Worked exam question 2

For a distribution of home prices in a county, the mean is 45 and the median is 54. Which is the most likely shape?

**Answer:** Skewed left

**Why:** A long left tail pulls the mean below the median.

**Working:** Mean 45 &lt; median 54: the mean is pulled toward the left tail.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Skewed right: The mean is pulled toward the tail.
- Symmetric: In a symmetric distribution, the mean and median are close.
- Uniform: A uniform distribution is symmetric, with mean near the median.

### Free-response practice: Comparing commute time (minutes)

Random samples of 15 were taken from two groups. The values of commute time (minutes) are:

**City A:** 12, 13, 14, 14, 16, 21, 21, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 29, 30, 30

**City B:** 15, 23, 25, 27, 35, 36, 36, 37, 38, 39, 39, 41, 43, 44, 60

**(a)** Find the five-number summary for each group.

To earn the point: City A correct. Model answer: 12, 14, 23, 27, 30

To earn the point: City B correct. Model answer: 15, 27, 37, 41, 60

**(b)** Use the 1.5 × IQR rule to determine whether City B has any outliers. Show your work.

To earn the point: Correct fences and conclusion. Model answer: IQR = 14; upper fence = 41 + 1.5(14) = 62. No outliers.

**(c)** Compare the distributions of the two groups (shape, center and variability) in context.

To earn the point: Compares centers with values. Model answer: The median for City B (37) is greater than for City A (23).

To earn the point: Compares variability with values. Model answer: City B is more variable (IQR 14 vs 13).

To earn the point: Comments on shape or unusual features. Model answer: Describes the shapes using the summaries.

**(d)** Which measure of center is more appropriate for City B, the mean or the median? Explain.

To earn the point: Median, because it is resistant to the outlier or skew. Model answer: Mean 35.9 is pulled toward the high value; median 37 is not.
