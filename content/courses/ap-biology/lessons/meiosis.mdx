Meiosis produces gametes with half the chromosome number, so fertilization restores the diploid number.

| | Mitosis | Meiosis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| divisions | one | two |
| products | 2 diploid, identical cells | 4 haploid, genetically different cells |
| homologs pair? | no | yes, in prophase I |
| purpose | growth, repair | sexual reproduction |

## The two divisions

- **Meiosis I** separates **homologous chromosomes**, halving the chromosome number.
- **Meiosis II** separates **sister chromatids**, like mitosis.

<Note title="Ploidy changes in meiosis I">
After meiosis I the cells are already haploid, even though each chromosome still has two chromatids.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Counting">
A cell with 2n = 8 undergoes meiosis. How many chromosomes are in each cell at the end of meiosis I and meiosis II?

1. Meiosis I separates homologs: 4 chromosomes (each with 2 chromatids).
2. Meiosis II separates chromatids: 4 chromosomes (single chromatids).

**Answer:** 4 and 4.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** An organism has a diploid number of 16. Ignoring crossing over, how many genetically different gametes can it produce by independent assortment?

**A.** 256. With 8 homologous pairs, there are 2^8 combinations. n = 8 pairs, each with two orientations at metaphase I: 2^8 = 256.

**Q.** Red and white snapdragons produce pink offspring. What pattern of inheritance is this?

**A.** Incomplete dominance. The heterozygote is intermediate.

**Q.** Hydrangeas with identical genotypes produce blue flowers in acidic soil and pink flowers in basic soil. What does this show?

**A.** The environment can affect the phenotype. Same genotype, different phenotypes.

**Q.** A dihybrid test cross produces 45% AB, 45% ab, 5% Ab and 5% aB offspring. What do these results suggest?

**A.** The genes are linked, about 10 map units apart. Recombinants are 10% of offspring.

**Q.** In which stage do homologous chromosomes separate?

**A.** Anaphase I of meiosis. Meiosis I separates homologs.

**Q.** Which trait is most likely inherited only through the mother?

**A.** A trait caused by a mitochondrial gene. Mitochondria are inherited from the egg.

**Q.** Nondisjunction of chromosome 21 in meiosis I produces gametes with which chromosome numbers (n = 23)?

**A.** Two with 24 and two with 22. One cell gets both homologs and the other none.
