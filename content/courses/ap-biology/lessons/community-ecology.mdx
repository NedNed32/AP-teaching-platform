**Simpson's diversity index** measures community diversity:

$$\text{SDI} = 1 - \sum \left(\frac{n}{N}\right)^2$$

Values near 1 indicate high diversity. **Keystone species** (such as sea otters, which control sea urchins and protect kelp forests) maintain community structure. **Ecological succession** changes communities over time after disturbances.

<Note title="Trophic cascades">
Changing one population can ripple through a food web. Removing wolves let elk increase, which reduced streamside plants.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Simpson's index">
A community has 6 individuals of species A, 3 of B and 1 of C. Calculate the diversity index.

1. N = 10. Proportions: 0.6, 0.3, 0.1.
2. Sum of squares: 0.36 + 0.09 + 0.01 = 0.46.
3. SDI = 1 − 0.46.

**Answer:** 0.54.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Individuals counted in a sample*

| Species | Count |
| --- | --- |
| A | 5 |
| B | 7 |
| C | 10 |
| D | 8 |

Using Simpson's diversity index, SDI = 1 − Σ(n/N)², what is the diversity of this sample?

**Answer:** 0.74

**Why:** N = 30; subtract the sum of squared proportions from 1.

**Working:** Proportions 0.17, 0.23, 0.33, 0.27; Σ(n/N)² = 0.26; SDI = 0.74.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 0.26: Subtract the sum from 1.
- 0.75: The species are not equally abundant; use each species' own proportion.
- 0.87: Do not divide the sum.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Why do invasive species often spread rapidly in new habitats?

**A.** They lack their natural predators, competitors and diseases. Without natural controls, they grow quickly.

**Q.** A relationship in which one species benefits and the other is unaffected is called:

**A.** Commensalism. One benefits, the other is neither helped nor harmed.

**Q.** Fertilizer runoff enters a lake, causing an algal bloom followed by fish deaths. What is the direct cause of the fish deaths?

**A.** Decomposers use up dissolved oxygen as the algae die. Eutrophication depletes oxygen.

**Q.** Which factor limits population growth in a density-dependent way?

**A.** Spread of a contagious disease. Disease spreads faster in crowded populations.

**Q.** Grassland A has 25 plant species; grassland B has 4. A drought kills drought-sensitive species. Which prediction is best supported?

**A.** Grassland A will recover productivity faster because some species likely tolerate drought. Biodiversity increases resilience.

**Q.** Why does energy, unlike matter, not cycle in ecosystems?

**A.** At each transfer, energy is lost as heat, which organisms cannot use. Energy flows one way.

**Q.** Pill bugs move faster and turn less in dry areas and slow down in moist areas, ending up mostly in moist areas. This behavior is:

**A.** Kinesis. A change in speed of random movement.

**Q.** Sea otters eat sea urchins, which eat kelp. When otters are removed, kelp forests disappear. Sea otters are best described as:

**A.** A keystone species. Their effect on the community is far larger than their numbers.
