## Gel electrophoresis

DNA is negatively charged and moves through a gel toward the positive end. **Small fragments move farther**. Comparing band patterns is used in forensics and paternity testing.

## PCR

1. Heat to separate strands.
2. Cool so primers bind.
3. Heat-stable DNA polymerase extends the primers.

Each cycle doubles the target DNA: after $$n$$ cycles, $$2^n$$ copies.

## Transformation and cloning

A gene is inserted into a plasmid, often with an antibiotic resistance gene; bacteria take up the plasmid. Growing them on antibiotics selects transformed cells, which can make the inserted protein (such as human insulin).

## Sequencing

DNA sequencing determines the order of bases, allowing comparison of genes and genomes.

<Note title="Reading a gel">
A child's bands must each match a band from one parent. A crime-scene sample matches a suspect only if every band lines up.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Fragment sizes">
A 1,000 base pair plasmid is cut at two sites, 300 base pairs apart. Which bands appear, and which moves farthest?

1. Two pieces: 300 bp and 700 bp.
2. Smaller fragments travel farther.

**Answer:** bands at 700 and 300 bp; the 300 bp band moves farthest.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A circular plasmid of 6000 base pairs is cut by a restriction enzyme at positions 1900 and 3700. The fragments are separated by gel electrophoresis. Which bands appear, and which travels farthest?

**Answer:** 1800 bp and 4200 bp; the 1800 bp band travels farthest

**Why:** Two cuts in a circle give two pieces; smaller fragments move farther.

**Working:** The fragments are 3700 − 1900 = 1800 bp and 6000 − 1800 = 4200 bp.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 1800 bp and 4200 bp; the 4200 bp band travels farthest: Smaller fragments move through the gel more easily.
- 1900 bp, 1800 bp and 2300 bp; the 1800 bp band travels farthest: A circular plasmid cut twice gives two fragments, not three.
- One 6000 bp band: The plasmid is cut into pieces.

### Worked exam question 2

A PCR reaction starts with 10 copyies of a target DNA sequence. Assuming each cycle doubles the DNA, how many copies are present after 28 cycles?

**Answer:** 10 × 2^28

**Why:** Each cycle doubles the number of copies.

**Working:** After 28 doublings: 10 × 2^28 copies.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 10 × 56: Copies double each cycle; they do not increase by 2.
- 10 × 28^2: Growth is exponential with base 2.
- 10 × 2^27: There are 28 doublings.
