Phylogenetic trees are **hypotheses** about evolutionary history, revised as new evidence appears.

## Reading trees

- Each **node** is a common ancestor.
- Taxa that share a more recent node are more closely related.
- Rotating branches around a node does not change the relationships.
- A **clade** is an ancestor and all of its descendants.

## Building trees

Group organisms by **shared derived characters**. The **outgroup** lacks the derived traits and roots the tree. Trees built from DNA sequences group species with the fewest differences together. The best tree usually requires the fewest evolutionary changes (**parsimony**).

<Note title="Not a ladder">
Living species are not ancestors of each other. Humans did not evolve from chimpanzees; both share a common ancestor.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Character table">
Lamprey: none of the traits. Trout: jaws. Frog: jaws, four limbs. Lizard: jaws, four limbs, amniotic egg. Which is the outgroup, and which two are most closely related?

1. The lamprey lacks all derived traits: outgroup.
2. Frog and lizard share the most derived traits.

**Answer:** lamprey is the outgroup; frog and lizard are most closely related among those listed.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

*Number of amino acid differences in a protein*

| Species pair | Differences |
| --- | --- |
| W and X | 3 |
| W and Y | 2 |
| W and Z | 23 |
| X and Y | 5 |
| X and Z | 26 |
| Y and Z | 18 |

Based on the data, which two species most likely share the most recent common ancestor among species W, X, Y, Z?

**Answer:** W and Y

**Why:** They have the fewest differences (2).

**Working:** Fewer sequence differences indicate more recent divergence: W and Y differ by only 2.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- W and X: They differ by 3, more than W and Y.
- W and Z: They differ by 23, more than W and Y.
- X and Y: They differ by 5, more than W and Y.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Why are genetically uniform crops vulnerable to disease?

**A.** If one plant is susceptible, nearly all are, because they share the same alleles. Low genetic diversity reduces resilience.

**Q.** After a mass extinction, the surviving groups often diversify rapidly. Why?

**A.** Many ecological niches become available. Adaptive radiation fills vacant niches.

**Q.** Plants often form new species within a single area when errors in meiosis double their chromosome number. This is:

**A.** Sympatric speciation by polyploidy. Polyploids are reproductively isolated from the parent species.

**Q.** Two populations of frogs live in the same pond but breed in different months. What type of barrier is this?

**A.** Temporal isolation, a prezygotic barrier. They are separated by breeding time.

**Q.** The forelimbs of humans, bats and whales have the same bone pattern but different functions. These are:

**A.** Homologous structures, evidence of common ancestry. Shared structure from a common ancestor.

**Q.** On a phylogenetic tree, what does a node represent?

**A.** The most recent common ancestor of the lineages that branch from it. Nodes are branch points.

**Q.** Which feature is shared by all living organisms, supporting common ancestry?

**A.** A nearly universal genetic code. All organisms read codons the same way.

**Q.** Which observation best supports the RNA world hypothesis?

**A.** Some RNA molecules can catalyze reactions as well as store information. RNA can serve both roles.
