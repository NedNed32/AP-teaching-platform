The cell cycle produces new cells for growth, repair and asexual reproduction.

## Phases

- **G1**: growth; normal functions. Cells may exit to **G0**, a non-dividing state.
- **S**: DNA replication; each chromosome becomes two sister chromatids.
- **G2**: preparation for division.
- **Mitosis**: prophase (chromosomes condense), metaphase (line up at the middle), anaphase (sister chromatids separate), telophase (nuclei re-form).
- **Cytokinesis**: a cleavage furrow (animals) or cell plate (plants) divides the cell.

## DNA content

If a G1 cell has an amount of DNA x, a G2 cell has 2x, and each daughter cell returns to x.

<Note title="Reading data">
Graphs of DNA per cell over time show a doubling in S phase and a halving at cytokinesis.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Counting chromatids">
A human cell (46 chromosomes) is in metaphase of mitosis. How many chromatids does it have?

1. Each of the 46 chromosomes has two sister chromatids after S phase.

**Answer:** 92 chromatids.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** A diploid cell has 16 chromosomes (2n = 16). How many chromatids are present in the cell during metaphase of mitosis?

**A.** 32. After S phase each chromosome has two sister chromatids. 16 chromosomes; chromatid count is 32 in metaphase of mitosis.

**Q.** A diploid cell has 8 chromosomes (2n = 8). How many chromatids are present in the cell during G1?
