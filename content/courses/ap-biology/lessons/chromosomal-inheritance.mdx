Genes are carried on chromosomes, so changes in chromosome number or structure affect many genes at once.

## Nondisjunction

If homologs fail to separate in meiosis I, or sister chromatids in meiosis II, some gametes get an extra copy and others lack one. Fertilization produces **trisomy** (2n + 1) or **monosomy** (2n − 1).

| Condition | Chromosomes |
| --- | --- |
| Down syndrome | trisomy 21 |
| Turner syndrome | XO |
| Klinefelter syndrome | XXY |

## Other changes

Deletions, duplications, inversions and translocations alter chromosome structure. Pedigrees and karyotypes help diagnose genetic disorders.

<Note title="Meiosis I versus II">
Nondisjunction in meiosis I makes all four gametes abnormal; in meiosis II, only two of the four are abnormal.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Predict gametes">
Nondisjunction occurs in meiosis I for one chromosome pair in a cell with n = 4. What chromosome numbers do the gametes have?

1. One daughter of meiosis I gets both homologs, the other none.
2. After meiosis II: two gametes with 5 and two with 3.

**Answer:** 5, 5, 3 and 3.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Red and white snapdragons produce pink offspring. What pattern of inheritance is this?

**A.** Incomplete dominance. The heterozygote is intermediate.

**Q.** Hydrangeas with identical genotypes produce blue flowers in acidic soil and pink flowers in basic soil. What does this show?

**A.** The environment can affect the phenotype. Same genotype, different phenotypes.

**Q.** A dihybrid test cross produces 45% AB, 45% ab, 5% Ab and 5% aB offspring. What do these results suggest?

**A.** The genes are linked, about 10 map units apart. Recombinants are 10% of offspring.

**Q.** In which stage do homologous chromosomes separate?

**A.** Anaphase I of meiosis. Meiosis I separates homologs.

**Q.** Which trait is most likely inherited only through the mother?

**A.** A trait caused by a mitochondrial gene. Mitochondria are inherited from the egg.

**Q.** Nondisjunction of chromosome 21 in meiosis I produces gametes with which chromosome numbers (n = 23)?

**A.** Two with 24 and two with 22. One cell gets both homologs and the other none.
