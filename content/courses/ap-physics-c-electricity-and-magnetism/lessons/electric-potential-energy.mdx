$$U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r} \qquad \Delta U = -W_{field}$$

For a system of charges, add $$U$$ for every pair. The work an external agent does to assemble the system equals its total potential energy.

Energy conservation: $$\Delta K = -\Delta U = -q\Delta V$$.

<Note title="Sign tells the story">
Positive U (like charges) means work was needed to push them together; negative U means the system is bound.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Speed from energy">
A proton is released from rest 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ m from another fixed proton. Find its speed when very far away.

1. $$U = \frac{(8.99 \times 10^9)(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2}{1.0 \times 10^{-10}} = 2.3 \times 10^{-18}$$ J.
2. $$v = \sqrt{2U/m} = \sqrt{2(2.3 \times 10^{-18})/(1.67 \times 10^{-27})}$$.

**Answer:** about $$5.2 \times 10^4$$ m/s.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

How much work must an external agent do to move a +5 µC charge slowly from a point at 86 V to a point at 26 V?

**Answer:** $$-3 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{J}$$

**Why:** W_ext = qΔV = q(V_b − V_a).

**Working:** W = (5 × 10⁻⁶)(26 − 86) = -3 \times 10^\{-4\} J.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$3 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{J}$$: ΔV is final minus initial.
- $$-300\ \mathrm{J}$$: Convert µC to C.
- $$5.6 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{J}$$: Use the difference in potential.

### Worked exam question 2

Charges of 5 µC, -5 µC, 2 µC sit at the corners of an equilateral triangle with sides 0.1 m. What is the system's electric potential energy?

**Answer:** $$-2.25\ \mathrm{J}$$

**Why:** Add kqᵢqⱼ/r for all three pairs.

**Working:** U = (k/s)(q₁q₂ + q₂q₃ + q₁q₃) = -2.25 J.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$-3.15\ \mathrm{J}$$: There are three pairs.
- $$-22.5\ \mathrm{J}$$: Potential energy uses 1/r.
- $$2.25\ \mathrm{J}$$: Keep the signs of the charges.
