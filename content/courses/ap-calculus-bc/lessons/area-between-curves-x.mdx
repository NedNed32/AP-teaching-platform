The integral of a function gives the area between its graph and the x-axis. Replace the x-axis with a second curve, and the same idea gives the area between two graphs.

<Figure src="assets/lessons/area-between.svg" alt="The line y = 2x above the parabola y = x squared between x = 0 and x = 2, with the region between them shaded." caption="The region between y = 2x and y = x² from x = 0 to x = 2." />

## The formula

If $$f(x) \geq g(x)$$ on $$[a, b]$$:

$$
\text{Area} = \int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)]\,dx = \int_a^b (\text{top} - \text{bottom})\,dx.
$$

Each vertical slice is a thin rectangle of height $$f(x) - g(x)$$ and width $$dx$$.

## Steps

1. Sketch the curves.
2. Find the intersection points; these are usually the limits.
3. Decide which curve is on top (test a point between the intersections).
4. Integrate top minus bottom.

<Note title="Store intersections on the calculator">
On calculator questions, store intersection values in calculator memory rather than retyping rounded decimals. Rounding the limits early can change the third decimal place of the area.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Area from the figure">
Find the area between $$y = 2x$$ and $$y = x^2$$.

1. Intersections: $$2x = x^2$$ gives $$x = 0$$ and $$x = 2$$.
2. At $$x = 1$$: $$2x = 2 > 1 = x^2$$, so the line is on top.
3. $$\int_0^2 (2x - x^2)\,dx = \left[x^2 - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = 4 - \frac{8}{3}$$.

**Answer:** $$\frac{4}{3}$$.
</WorkedExample>

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $$y = x^2$$ and $$y = 3x$$?

**A.** $$\frac{9}{2}$$. $$\int_0^{3} (3x - x^2)\,dx$$: top curve minus bottom curve. The curves meet at $$0$$ and $$3$$. Area $$= \int_0^{3} (3u - u^2)\,du = \frac{3u^2}{2} - \frac{u^3}{3}\Big|_0^{3} = \frac{9}{2}$$.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $$y = x^2$$ and $$y = 2x$$?

**A.** $$\frac{4}{3}$$. $$\int_0^{2} (2x - x^2)\,dx$$: top curve minus bottom curve. The curves meet at $$0$$ and $$2$$. Area $$= \int_0^{2} (2u - u^2)\,du = \frac{2u^2}{2} - \frac{u^3}{3}\Big|_0^{2} = \frac{4}{3}$$.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the curves $$x = y^2$$ and $$x = y$$?

**A.** $$\frac{1}{6}$$. $$\int_0^{1} (y - y^2)\,dy$$: right curve minus left curve. The curves meet at $$0$$ and $$1$$. Area $$= \int_0^{1} (u - u^2)\,du = \frac{u^2}{2} - \frac{u^3}{3}\Big|_0^{1} = \frac{1}{6}$$.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the curves $$x = y^2$$ and $$x = 2y$$?

**A.** $$\frac{4}{3}$$. $$\int_0^{2} (2y - y^2)\,dy$$: right curve minus left curve. The curves meet at $$0$$ and $$2$$. Area $$= \int_0^{2} (2u - u^2)\,du = \frac{2u^2}{2} - \frac{u^3}{3}\Big|_0^{2} = \frac{4}{3}$$.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $$y = x^2$$ and $$y = x$$?

**A.** $$\frac{1}{6}$$. $$\int_0^{1} (x - x^2)\,dx$$: top curve minus bottom curve. The curves meet at $$0$$ and $$1$$. Area $$= \int_0^{1} (u - u^2)\,du = \frac{u^2}{2} - \frac{u^3}{3}\Big|_0^{1} = \frac{1}{6}$$.

**Q.** What is the area of the region enclosed by the graphs of $$y = 4\cos\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)$$ and $$y = x^2 - 1$$?

**A.** $$12.257$$. The curves meet at $$x \approx -1.847$$ and $$x \approx 1.847$$; integrate top minus bottom. Store the intersection x-values from the calculator, then $$\int_{-1.847}^{1.847} \left(4\cos\frac{x}{2} - (x^2 - 1)\right)dx \approx 12.257$$.

### Free-response practice: Area and volume

Let $$R$$ be the region enclosed by the graphs of $$f(x) = 4\cos\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)$$ and $$g(x) = x^2 - 2$$.

**(a)** Find the area of $$R$$.

To earn the point: Finds the limits of integration (the intersection points). Model answer: $$x \approx -2.028$$ and $$x \approx 2.028$$

To earn the point: Writes the integral of top minus bottom. Model answer: $$\int_{-2.028}^{2.028} (f(x) - g(x))\,dx$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct area. Model answer: $$16.135$$

**(b)** Find the volume of the solid generated when $$R$$ is revolved about the horizontal line $$y = -3$$.

To earn the point: Uses the washer method with radii measured from the line. Model answer: $$\pi\int_{-2.028}^{2.028} \left[(f(x) + 3)^2 - (g(x) + 3)^2\right]dx$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct volume. Model answer: $$426.973$$

**(c)** The region $$R$$ is the base of a solid. Cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis are squares. Find the volume of the solid.

To earn the point: Writes the integral of the side length squared. Model answer: $$\int_{-2.028}^{2.028} (f(x) - g(x))^2\,dx$$

To earn the point: Gives the correct volume. Model answer: $$77.261$$
