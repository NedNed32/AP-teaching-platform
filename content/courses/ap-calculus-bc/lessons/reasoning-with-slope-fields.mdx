Slope fields are also read in reverse: given a picture, identify the equation, or follow the segments to trace a solution.

## Matching a field to an equation

Test a few easy points, especially on the axes:

1. **Where are the slopes zero?** Along a vertical line (depends on $$x$$), a horizontal line (depends on $$y$$), or a slanted line?
2. **Are columns or rows parallel?** That tells you whether only $$x$$ or only $$y$$ appears.
3. **Check signs** in each quadrant, and eliminate the answer choices that disagree.

## Sketching a solution curve

Start at the given point and move left and right, staying tangent to the nearby segments. A solution curve:

- never crosses an equilibrium solution (for the equations in this course),
- flattens out where the segments are horizontal,
- is unique through each point.

## Long-term behavior

Equilibrium solutions can attract or repel. For $$\frac{dy}{dt} = 2 - y$$, solutions above $$y = 2$$ decrease toward it, and those below increase toward it, so every solution approaches 2 as $$t \to \infty$$.

<Note title="Use the equation to back up the picture">
On free-response questions, justify claims about solution curves with the equation itself: "Since $$\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$$ when $$y = 2$$, the line $$y = 2$$ is a solution."
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Eliminating answer choices">
A slope field has horizontal segments along the y-axis and negative slopes everywhere to the right of it, at every height. Which equation fits: $$\frac{dy}{dx} = -x$$, $$\frac{dy}{dx} = -y$$, or $$\frac{dy}{dx} = x - y$$?

1. Horizontal along $$x = 0$$ means the slope is 0 whenever $$x = 0$$, at every height. $$-y$$ and $$x - y$$ are not 0 at $$(0, 1)$$.
2. $$-x$$ is 0 on the y-axis and negative for $$x > 0$$.

**Answer:** $$\frac{dy}{dx} = -x$$.
</WorkedExample>

**Q.** Which of the following differential equations could have the slope field shown?

**A.** $$\frac{dy}{dx} = x$$. Check where the segments are horizontal and where the slopes are positive or negative; this equation matches at every point. Compare slopes at specific points. For example, at $$(1, 0)$$ the field's segment has slope $$1$$, and at $$(0, 1)$$ slope $$0$$, which matches $$\frac{dy}{dx} = x$$.

**Q.** Let $$y = f(x)$$ be the solution to $$\frac{dy}{dx} = x^2 - y$$ passing through the point $$(-2, -2)$$. What is $$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}$$ at that point?

**A.** $$-10$$. $$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 2x - \frac{dy}{dx}$$, and $$\frac{dy}{dx} = 6$$ at the point. Differentiate implicitly: $$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 2x - \frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - (x^2 - y)$$. At $$(-2, -2)$$: $$-4 - 6 = -10$$.
