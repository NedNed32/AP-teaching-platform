Geometric series are the one family where you can always find the exact sum. They are also the template for power series, so they matter far beyond this topic.

## The formula

For $$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} ar^n$$:

$$
S = \frac{a}{1 - r} \quad\text{if } |r| < 1, \qquad\text{diverges if } |r| \geq 1.
$$

**Why:** $$S_N - rS_N = a - ar^{N+1}$$, so $$S_N = \frac{a(1 - r^{N+1})}{1 - r}$$, and $$r^{N+1} \to 0$$ exactly when $$|r| < 1$$.

## Identifying a and r

- $$a$$ is the **first term**, whatever the starting index.
- $$r$$ is the ratio of consecutive terms.

$$
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{3}{4^n} = \frac{3}{16} + \frac{3}{64} + \cdots, \qquad a = \frac{3}{16},\; r = \frac{1}{4},\; S = \frac{3/16}{3/4} = \frac{1}{4}.
$$

<Note title="Check the starting index">
Plugging into $$\frac{a}{1 - r}$$ with the wrong first term is the most common mistake. Write out the first two terms before using the formula.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="A repeating decimal">
Write $$0.\overline{36}$$ as a fraction.

1. $$0.363636\ldots = \frac{36}{100} + \frac{36}{100^2} + \cdots$$.
2. $$a = \frac{36}{100}$$, $$r = \frac{1}{100}$$.
3. $$S = \frac{36/100}{99/100} = \frac{36}{99}$$.

**Answer:** $$\frac{4}{11}$$.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

What is the sum of $$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} 5\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^n$$?

**Answer:** $$10$$

**Why:** Geometric with first term $$\frac{10}{3}$$ and ratio $$\frac{2}{3}$$.

**Working:** $$|r| = \left|\frac{2}{3}\right| < 1$$, so the series converges to $$\frac{\frac{10}{3}}{1 - \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)} = 10$$.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- $$15$$: The first term depends on the starting index; check the term for the first value of n.
- $$3$$: The formula is $$\frac{a}{1 - r}$$, not $$\frac{a}{1 + r}$$.
- $$5$$: This divides by $$r$$ instead of $$1 - r$$.
