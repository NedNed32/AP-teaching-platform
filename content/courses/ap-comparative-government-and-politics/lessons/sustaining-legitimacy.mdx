Regimes sustain legitimacy through:

- **Policy performance**: growth, social spending, security.
- **Elections and participation**, even if limited.
- **Nationalism** and appeals to identity.
- **Co-optation** of elites and groups.
- **Coercion**: censorship, surveillance, arrests.

Resource wealth can fund benefits (Russia, Iran, Nigeria), but price drops strain budgets and legitimacy.

<Note title="Mixed strategies">
Authoritarian regimes usually combine benefits for supporters with pressure on opponents.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Explain">
How might falling oil prices threaten a rentier state's legitimacy?

1. Revenue falls, so spending and subsidies are cut.
2. Citizens may become dissatisfied.

**Answer:** less revenue means fewer benefits, which can increase discontent.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Democracy index score (0–10, higher = more democratic)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 2.1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Country B | 6.1 | 6.2 | 6.4 | 6.5 | 6.6 |
| Country C | 7.6 | 7.2 | 7 | 6.8 | 6.4 |
| Country D | 5.9 | 6.2 | 6.5 | 6.7 | 7 |

Which country's value changed the most between 2004 and 2024?

**Answer:** Country C

**Why:** It changed by -1.2, the largest change in either direction.

**Working:** Changes 2004–2024: Country A -0.1, Country B +0.5, Country C -1.2, Country D +1.1.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its change was -0.1.
- Country B: Its change was +0.5.
- Country D: Its change was +1.1.

### Worked exam question 3

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Democracy index score (0–10, higher = more democratic)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 3.7 | 4.1 | 4.3 | 4.7 | 4.9 |
| Country B | 8.8 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| Country C | 3.5 | 3.5 | 3.4 | 3.3 | 3.4 |
| Country D | 6.2 | 6.1 | 6.1 | 6 | 5.9 |

Which conclusion about Country A is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 3.7 to 4.9 is consistent with democratization

**Why:** The value rose over the period.

**Working:** Country A: 3.7 → 4.1 → 4.3 → 4.7 → 4.9.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 3.7 to 4.9 is consistent with democratic backsliding: Read the direction of the change.
- Country A had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country A is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

### Worked exam question 4

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Citizens who trust the national government (%)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 57 | 52 | 48 | 44 | 38 |
| Country B | 20 | 25 | 31 | 35 | 39 |
| Country C | 30 | 32 | 36 | 39 | 41 |
| Country D | 58 | 56 | 54 | 53 | 51 |

Which conclusion about Country B is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 20 to 39 is consistent with rising legitimacy

**Why:** The value rose over the period.

**Working:** Country B: 20 → 25 → 31 → 35 → 39.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 20 to 39 is consistent with declining legitimacy: Read the direction of the change.
- Country B had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country B is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which is an example of a normative statement?

**A.** The government should reduce income inequality. It expresses a value judgment.

**Q.** Which best describes a state?

**A.** A political organization with sovereignty over a territory and population. That is the definition of a state.

**Q.** A government relies mainly on rapid economic growth to justify its rule. What source of legitimacy is this?

**A.** Performance legitimacy. Legitimacy based on results.

**Q.** What distinguishes a regime from a government?

**A.** A regime is the set of fundamental rules and institutions; a government is the people currently in power. Regimes outlast governments.

**Q.** Which is most characteristic of an illiberal democracy?

**A.** Elections are held, but civil liberties and independent institutions are weak. Elections without full liberal protections.

**Q.** Which indicator would most strongly suggest declining regime legitimacy?

**A.** Sustained mass protests and falling trust in institutions. These signal citizens no longer accept the government's right to rule.
