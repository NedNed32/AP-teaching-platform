Young populations need jobs and schools; aging populations need pensions and health care.

<Note title="Youth bulge">
A large young population can be an economic opportunity if jobs exist, or a source of instability if they do not.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Explain">
Why might a very young, fast-growing population challenge Nigeria's government?

1. Large demand for jobs, schools and services.

**Answer:** if growth outpaces job creation, unemployment and unrest may rise.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Trade as a share of GDP (%)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 42 | 47 | 54 | 60 | 67 |
| Country B | 85 | 90 | 90 | 90 | 90 |
| Country C | 20 | 24 | 29 | 32 | 35 |
| Country D | 22 | 25 | 26 | 30 | 33 |

Which country had the highest value in 2024?

**Answer:** Country B

**Why:** Its 2024 value (90) is the highest.

**Working:** 2024 values: Country A 67, Country B 90, Country C 35, Country D 33.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its 2024 value is lower.
- Country C: Its 2024 value is lower.
- Country D: Its 2024 value is lower.

### Worked exam question 2

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Urban population (%)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 35 | 30 | 26 | 21 | 20 |
| Country B | 54 | 56 | 57 | 57 | 58 |
| Country C | 37 | 34 | 29 | 24 | 20 |
| Country D | 26 | 25 | 22 | 20 | 20 |

Which country's value changed the most between 2004 and 2024?

**Answer:** Country C

**Why:** It changed by -17, the largest change in either direction.

**Working:** Changes 2004–2024: Country A -15, Country B +4, Country C -17, Country D -6.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its change was -15.
- Country B: Its change was +4.
- Country D: Its change was -6.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries began gradual market reforms in 1978 while keeping one-party rule?

**A.** China. China's reform era began in 1978.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries is a member of a free trade agreement with the United States and Canada (USMCA)?

**A.** Mexico. Mexico joined NAFTA in 1994, replaced by USMCA in 2020.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries replaced a one-child policy with policies encouraging more births?

**A.** China. China moved to two-child (2016) and three-child (2021) policies.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries relies heavily on oil exports and has experienced conflict over oil revenues in its Delta region?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's Niger Delta has seen conflict over oil.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries rapidly privatized state industries in the 1990s, creating powerful oligarchs?

**A.** Russia. Russia's 1990s privatization concentrated wealth among insiders.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries left the European Union in 2020?

**A.** United Kingdom. Brexit took effect in 2020.

**Q.** What is the resource curse?

**A.** The tendency of resource-rich states to experience corruption, weak institutions and slower development. Resource wealth can undermine governance.

**Q.** Which is a likely challenge for a state with a rapidly aging population?

**A.** Rising pension and health costs with fewer workers to pay taxes. The dependency ratio rises.

**Q.** Why is the European Union described as supranational?

**A.** Its laws and institutions can bind member states. Members pool sovereignty.

**Q.** Which is an example of economic liberalization?

**A.** Selling state-owned companies to private investors. Privatization reduces state control.

**Q.** Which is a common political backlash to globalization?

**A.** Nationalist movements seeking to restore sovereignty and limit immigration. Brexit is an example.
