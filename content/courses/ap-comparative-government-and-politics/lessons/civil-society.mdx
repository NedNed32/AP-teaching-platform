A strong civil society links citizens to government, builds trust and checks state power.

- **UK**: a large, independent civil society.
- **Mexico, Nigeria**: active civil society facing violence or corruption.
- **Russia**: "foreign agent" laws restrict NGOs and media.
- **China**: the state permits groups that serve its goals; independent organizing is limited.
- **Iran**: tight control, with periodic protest movements.

<Note title="State-sponsored groups">
Authoritarian states often create official organizations that look like civil society but are controlled by the state.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Explain">
How can a strong civil society support democracy?

1. It lets citizens organize, express interests and hold officials accountable.

**Answer:** by channeling participation and checking government power.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Adults who joined a protest in the past year (%)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 15 | 13 | 11 | 9 | 6 |
| Country B | 2 | 5 | 7 | 10 | 12 |
| Country C | 20 | 20 | 21 | 21 | 20 |
| Country D | 18 | 16 | 14 | 11 | 10 |

Which conclusion about Country A is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 15 to 6 is consistent with falling unconventional participation

**Why:** The value fell over the period.

**Working:** Country A: 15 → 13 → 11 → 9 → 6.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 15 to 6 is consistent with rising unconventional participation: Read the direction of the change.
- Country A had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country A is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries requires many NGOs and media receiving foreign support to register as 'foreign agents'?

**A.** Russia. Russia's foreign agent laws restrict civil society.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries faced a 2014 independence referendum in one of its constituent nations?

**A.** United Kingdom. Scotland voted on independence in 2014.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a constitutional 'federal character' principle requiring appointments to reflect national diversity?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's federal character principle aims to balance groups.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has major overlapping ethnic, religious and regional cleavages between a mainly Muslim north and a mainly Christian south?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's north–south cleavage combines religion, ethnicity and region.

**Q.** Which ideology emphasizes free markets, privatization and reduced state intervention?

**A.** Neoliberalism. That defines neoliberalism.

**Q.** Freedom of the press is best classified as:

**A.** A civil liberty. A protection from government interference.

**Q.** Why are coinciding cleavages more likely to cause conflict than cross-cutting cleavages?

**A.** Divisions reinforce each other, so groups share fewer identities. Overlapping divisions deepen conflict.

**Q.** Which is an example of civil society?

**A.** An independent environmental organization. Voluntary groups outside the state.

**Q.** Which agent of political socialization do authoritarian states most often control directly?

**A.** State media and the education system. These shape beliefs at scale.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Nigeria is accurate?

**A.** Nigeria has a constitutional 'federal character' principle requiring appointments to reflect national diversity. Nigeria's federal character principle aims to balance groups.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about United Kingdom is accurate?

**A.** United Kingdom faced a 2014 independence referendum in one of its constituent nations. Scotland voted on independence in 2014.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Russia is accurate?

**A.** Russia requires many NGOs and media receiving foreign support to register as 'foreign agents'. Russia's foreign agent laws restrict civil society.

### Free-response practice: Conceptual analysis: rentier state

Answer parts A, B, C and D about rentier state.

**(a)** Define rentier state.

To earn the point: An accurate definition. Model answer: A state that earns much of its revenue from natural resources rather than taxation.

**(b)** Describe one way a government can strengthen or use rentier state.

To earn the point: An accurate description. Model answer: Using resource revenue to fund subsidies and public jobs that build support.

**(c)** Explain one way rentier state can be weakened or create challenges for a government.

To earn the point: An accurate explanation. Model answer: Falling world prices cut revenue and can force unpopular spending cuts.

**(d)** Explain how rentier state can affect political stability or accountability.

To earn the point: A logical explanation linking the concept to stability or accountability. Model answer: Because the state relies less on taxes, citizens may have less leverage to demand accountability.
