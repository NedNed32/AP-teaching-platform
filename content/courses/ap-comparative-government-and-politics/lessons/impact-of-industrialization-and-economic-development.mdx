Industrialization brings urbanization, a larger middle class, rising education and new demands on the state.

Measures of development: **GDP per capita**, **HDI**, **Gini coefficient**, poverty rates.

- **China**: rapid growth lifted hundreds of millions out of poverty but increased inequality and pollution.
- **Mexico**: manufacturing growth, persistent regional inequality.
- **Nigeria**: large population, high poverty despite oil wealth.

**Modernization theory** argues development promotes democracy; China is a key counterexample.

<Note title="Read indicators correctly">
GDP per capita measures average income; HDI adds health and education; Gini measures how unequally income is shared.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Interpret">
A country's GDP per capita doubled while its Gini rose from 0.30 to 0.45. What happened?

1. Average income rose.
2. Inequality increased.

**Answer:** the economy grew, but the gains were shared unequally.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical development indicators.

*Development indicators*

| Country | GDP per capita (US$) | HDI | Gini coefficient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 13,000 | 0.82 | 0.27 |
| Country B | 6,000 | 0.7 | 0.42 |
| Country C | 32,000 | 0.53 | 0.43 |
| Country D | 5,000 | 0.88 | 0.32 |

Which country has the highest level of human development as measured by the HDI?

**Answer:** Country D

**Why:** Its HDI (0.88) is highest; HDI combines health, education and income.

**Working:** HDI values: Country A 0.82, Country B 0.7, Country C 0.53, Country D 0.88.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its HDI is lower.
- Country B: Its HDI is lower.
- Country C: Its HDI is lower.

### Worked exam question 2

The table shows hypothetical development indicators.

*Development indicators*

| Country | GDP per capita (US$) | HDI | Gini coefficient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 11,000 | 0.62 | 0.26 |
| Country B | 29,000 | 0.46 | 0.4 |
| Country C | 17,000 | 0.63 | 0.38 |
| Country D | 22,000 | 0.58 | 0.44 |

Which country has the most unequal distribution of income?

**Answer:** Country D

**Why:** It has the highest Gini coefficient (0.44).

**Working:** Higher Gini means more inequality: Country A 0.26, Country B 0.4, Country C 0.38, Country D 0.44.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its Gini coefficient is lower.
- Country B: The highest GDP per capita does not mean the most inequality.
- Country C: Its Gini coefficient is lower.

### Worked exam question 3

The table shows hypothetical development indicators.

*Development indicators*

| Country | GDP per capita (US$) | HDI | Gini coefficient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 45,000 | 0.51 | 0.52 |
| Country B | 17,000 | 0.64 | 0.39 |
| Country C | 17,000 | 0.86 | 0.31 |
| Country D | 11,000 | 0.71 | 0.38 |

Which statement about what these indicators measure is accurate?

**Answer:** GDP per capita measures average income, while the Gini coefficient measures how unequally income is distributed

**Why:** They measure different things.

**Working:** GDP per capita = average income; HDI = health, education and income; Gini = inequality (0 equal, 1 unequal).

**Mistakes to avoid**

- A higher Gini coefficient means a more equal society: Higher Gini means more inequality.
- HDI measures only income: HDI also includes life expectancy and education.
- GDP per capita shows how income is shared among citizens: It is an average and says nothing about distribution.
