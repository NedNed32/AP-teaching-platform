| Country | Term rules |
| --- | --- |
| Mexico | one six-year term (sexenio) |
| Nigeria | four-year terms, two-term limit |
| Iran | four-year terms; two consecutive terms |
| Russia | six-year terms; 2020 amendments reset previous terms for the incumbent |
| China | presidential term limits removed in 2018 |
| UK | no PM term limit; elections at least every five years |

Term limits prevent personal concentration of power and encourage turnover; removing them can signal authoritarian consolidation.

<Note title="Why leaders change term limits">
Changing term limits allows an incumbent to stay in power, reducing alternation and accountability.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Explain the significance">
What did Russia's 2020 constitutional amendments mean for presidential term limits?

1. The incumbent's previous terms were reset to zero.

**Answer:** they allowed the sitting president to run for additional terms, extending personal rule.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries adopted a reform in 2024 to have judges, including Supreme Court justices, chosen by popular election?

**A.** Mexico. Mexico's 2024 judicial reform introduced popular election of judges.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has an unelected council that can veto legislation and disqualify candidates?

**A.** Iran. Iran's Guardian Council vets laws and candidates.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a presidential system in which the president is both head of state and head of government?

**A.** Nigeria. Mexico and Nigeria have presidential systems.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a semi-presidential system with both a president and a prime minister?

**A.** Russia. Russia's president appoints a prime minister, with Duma approval.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries limits its president to a single six-year term?

**A.** Mexico. Mexico's president serves one six-year term (sexenio).

**Q.** Which of the following course countries removed presidential term limits in 2018?

**A.** China. China's 2018 constitutional amendment removed the two-term limit.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries limits its president to a lifetime maximum of two four-year terms?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's president may serve two four-year terms.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries amended its constitution in 2020 in a way that reset the incumbent president's previous terms?

**A.** Russia. Russia's 2020 amendments reset the count for the incumbent.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has its highest leader chosen and formally overseen by an elected assembly of clerics?

**A.** Iran. Iran's Assembly of Experts chooses the Supreme Leader.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has an upper house whose members are appointed, hereditary or bishops rather than elected?

**A.** United Kingdom. The House of Lords is not elected.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a unicameral national legislature?

**A.** Iran. China's NPC and Iran's Majles are unicameral.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a parliamentary system in which the executive is drawn from the legislature?

**A.** United Kingdom. The UK prime minister and cabinet come from Parliament.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has courts that cannot strike down acts of the national legislature, because of legislative sovereignty?

**A.** United Kingdom. Parliamentary sovereignty means UK courts cannot overturn Acts of Parliament.

### Free-response practice: Comparative analysis: electoral systems for the legislature

Answer parts A, B and C, comparing Mexico and Nigeria.

**(a)** Define electoral systems for the legislature.

To earn the point: Accurate definition. Model answer: The rules that translate votes into legislative seats.

**(b)** Describe electoral systems for the legislature in Mexico and in Nigeria.

To earn the point: Accurate description for Mexico. Model answer: Mixed: 300 district seats and 200 proportional seats in the Chamber of Deputies.

To earn the point: Accurate description for Nigeria. Model answer: Single-member-district plurality for the House of Representatives.

**(c)** Explain a reason for the difference (or similarity) between Mexico and Nigeria with respect to electoral systems for the legislature.

To earn the point: A reason linked to regime type, history or political goals. Model answer: Countries choose rules to balance stable majorities with fair representation, or, in authoritarian systems, to advantage ruling parties.
