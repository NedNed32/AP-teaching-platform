Judicial independence is supported by long or secure terms, appointment processes that limit political control, and respect for court rulings.

- **UK**: strong independence; separation from Parliament since 2009.
- **Mexico**: debate over the 2024 reform replacing appointed judges with popularly elected ones.
- **Nigeria**: courts sometimes rule against governments but face corruption and pressure.
- **Russia, China, Iran**: courts largely serve the regime.

<Note title="Why it matters">
Independent courts protect civil liberties and property rights and make rule of law credible.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Evaluate">
A court cancels an election result for fraud, and the result is accepted. What does this show?

1. Courts can check powerful actors and are obeyed.

**Answer:** a degree of judicial independence and rule of law.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Major court rulings against the government (per year)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 12 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 |
| Country B | 6 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 0 |
| Country C | 11 | 9 | 8 | 6 | 4 |
| Country D | 5 | 3 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

Which conclusion about Country C is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 11 to 4 is consistent with declining judicial independence

**Why:** The value fell over the period.

**Working:** Country C: 11 → 9 → 8 → 6 → 4.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 11 to 4 is consistent with greater judicial independence: Read the direction of the change.
- Country C had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country C is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries adopted a reform in 2024 to have judges, including Supreme Court justices, chosen by popular election?

**A.** Mexico. Mexico's 2024 judicial reform introduced popular election of judges.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has an unelected council that can veto legislation and disqualify candidates?

**A.** Iran. Iran's Guardian Council vets laws and candidates.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a presidential system in which the president is both head of state and head of government?

**A.** Nigeria. Mexico and Nigeria have presidential systems.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a semi-presidential system with both a president and a prime minister?

**A.** Russia. Russia's president appoints a prime minister, with Duma approval.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries limits its president to a single six-year term?

**A.** Mexico. Mexico's president serves one six-year term (sexenio).

**Q.** Which of the following course countries removed presidential term limits in 2018?

**A.** China. China's 2018 constitutional amendment removed the two-term limit.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries limits its president to a lifetime maximum of two four-year terms?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's president may serve two four-year terms.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries amended its constitution in 2020 in a way that reset the incumbent president's previous terms?

**A.** Russia. Russia's 2020 amendments reset the count for the incumbent.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has its highest leader chosen and formally overseen by an elected assembly of clerics?

**A.** Iran. Iran's Assembly of Experts chooses the Supreme Leader.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has an upper house whose members are appointed, hereditary or bishops rather than elected?

**A.** United Kingdom. The House of Lords is not elected.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a unicameral national legislature?

**A.** Iran. China's NPC and Iran's Majles are unicameral.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a parliamentary system in which the executive is drawn from the legislature?

**A.** United Kingdom. The UK prime minister and cabinet come from Parliament.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has courts that cannot strike down acts of the national legislature, because of legislative sovereignty?

**A.** United Kingdom. Parliamentary sovereignty means UK courts cannot overturn Acts of Parliament.

**Q.** Which is an advantage of a parliamentary system with a single-party majority?

**A.** The government can pass its legislative program efficiently. Fusion of powers gives the majority control.

**Q.** Which feature most strengthens judicial independence?

**A.** Judges have secure tenure and cannot be easily removed for their rulings. Security protects courts from pressure.

**Q.** A legislature approves nearly every bill proposed by the executive and rarely questions officials. This legislature is best described as:

**A.** A rubber-stamp legislature with little independence. It does not check the executive.

**Q.** What is a vote of no confidence?

**A.** A legislative vote that can force a parliamentary government to resign. It holds the executive accountable.

**Q.** What does divided government mean in a presidential system?

**A.** The presidency and the legislature are controlled by different parties. This can produce gridlock.
