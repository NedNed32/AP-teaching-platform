## Indicators of democratization

- Competitive, free and fair elections, with peaceful **alternation** of power.
- Independent election commissions and courts.
- Expanded civil liberties, free media and active civil society.

## Course examples

- **Mexico**: decades of PRI dominance ended in 2000; an independent electoral authority (now the INE) supervises elections.
- **Nigeria**: return to civilian rule in 1999; in 2015 an incumbent president lost and accepted defeat, the first such transfer.
- **Russia**: partial democratization in the 1990s followed by backsliding.

Consolidation is helped by economic development, a strong civil society and elite acceptance of the rules.

<Note title="Backsliding">
Democratization is not one-way. Weakened courts, controlled media and changed term limits can reverse earlier gains.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Evidence of consolidation">
Which event best shows democratic consolidation: a new constitution, or the second peaceful transfer of power between rival parties after a lost election?

1. Consolidation means the rules are accepted by losers as well as winners.

**Answer:** the peaceful transfer of power after an election loss.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Democracy index score (0–10, higher = more democratic)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 2.1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Country B | 6.1 | 6.2 | 6.4 | 6.5 | 6.6 |
| Country C | 7.6 | 7.2 | 7 | 6.8 | 6.4 |
| Country D | 5.9 | 6.2 | 6.5 | 6.7 | 7 |

Which country's value changed the most between 2004 and 2024?

**Answer:** Country C

**Why:** It changed by -1.2, the largest change in either direction.

**Working:** Changes 2004–2024: Country A -0.1, Country B +0.5, Country C -1.2, Country D +1.1.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Country A: Its change was -0.1.
- Country B: Its change was +0.5.
- Country D: Its change was +1.1.

### Worked exam question 3

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Democracy index score (0–10, higher = more democratic)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 3.7 | 4.1 | 4.3 | 4.7 | 4.9 |
| Country B | 8.8 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| Country C | 3.5 | 3.5 | 3.4 | 3.3 | 3.4 |
| Country D | 6.2 | 6.1 | 6.1 | 6 | 5.9 |

Which conclusion about Country A is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 3.7 to 4.9 is consistent with democratization

**Why:** The value rose over the period.

**Working:** Country A: 3.7 → 4.1 → 4.3 → 4.7 → 4.9.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 3.7 to 4.9 is consistent with democratic backsliding: Read the direction of the change.
- Country A had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country A is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

### Worked exam question 4

The table shows hypothetical data for four countries.

*Citizens who trust the national government (%)*

| Country | 2004 | 2009 | 2014 | 2019 | 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country A | 57 | 52 | 48 | 44 | 38 |
| Country B | 20 | 25 | 31 | 35 | 39 |
| Country C | 30 | 32 | 36 | 39 | 41 |
| Country D | 58 | 56 | 54 | 53 | 51 |

Which conclusion about Country B is best supported by the data?

**Answer:** The change from 20 to 39 is consistent with rising legitimacy

**Why:** The value rose over the period.

**Working:** Country B: 20 → 25 → 31 → 35 → 39.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- The change from 20 to 39 is consistent with declining legitimacy: Read the direction of the change.
- Country B had the highest value of the four countries in every year: Check the other countries' values.
- The data prove that Country B is a liberal democracy: One indicator cannot establish a regime type.

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's Fourth Republic began in 1999.

### Free-response practice: Argument essay

Develop an argument as to whether economic liberalization promotes democratization.

In your response, you should do the following: respond with a defensible claim or thesis that establishes a line of reasoning; support your claim with at least two pieces of specific and relevant evidence from one or more course countries; use reasoning to explain why your evidence supports your claim; and respond to an opposing or alternative perspective using refutation, concession or rebuttal.

**(a)** Write your essay.

To earn the point: Thesis: a defensible claim that establishes a line of reasoning (1 point). Model answer: Economic liberalization does not necessarily promote democratization; regimes can liberalize markets while keeping political control.

To earn the point: Evidence: two pieces of specific, relevant evidence from course countries (up to 2 points). Model answer: China's market reforms since 1978 produced rapid growth while the CCP maintained one-party rule. Mexico's economic opening in the 1980s and 1990s coincided with growing political competition and the PRI's 2000 defeat.

To earn the point: Reasoning: explains why the evidence supports the claim (1 point). Model answer: Connects each example back to the thesis rather than just describing it.

To earn the point: Alternative perspective: responds with refutation, concession or rebuttal (1 point). Model answer: Some argue a growing middle class demands rights, as in Mexico; but China shows performance legitimacy can sustain authoritarian rule.
