| Federal | Unitary |
| --- | --- |
| Mexico (32 federal entities) | United Kingdom (with devolution) |
| Nigeria (36 states plus a capital territory) | China |
| Russia (federal subjects) | Iran |

Federalism can manage diversity (ethnic, regional) but can also create conflict over resources. In practice, Russia's federalism is highly centralized.

<Note title="On paper versus in practice">
A constitution may say federal, but power can be concentrated at the center, as in Russia.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Classify">
In a country, regional governments' powers are guaranteed by the constitution and cannot be removed by a simple national law. Federal or unitary?

1. Constitutionally protected regional authority.

**Answer:** federal.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has a federal system?

**A.** Russia. Mexico, Nigeria and Russia are federal; the UK, China and Iran are unitary.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries returned to civilian rule in 1999 after decades of military governments?

**A.** Nigeria. Nigeria's Fourth Republic began in 1999.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries is a one-party state in which the ruling party's leadership is written into the constitution?

**A.** China. China's constitution affirms the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries saw a dominant party lose the presidency in 2000 after more than 70 years in power?

**A.** Mexico. The PRI lost the presidency in 2000.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries has an uncodified constitution?

**A.** United Kingdom. The UK constitution is not contained in a single document.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries is a unitary state that has devolved power to regional legislatures?

**A.** United Kingdom. The UK devolved power to Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland but remains unitary.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries is a theocratic republic in which a cleric holds the highest authority?

**A.** Iran. Iran's Supreme Leader, a senior cleric, is the highest authority.

**Q.** Which of the following course countries is generally classified as a consolidated liberal democracy?

**A.** United Kingdom. Among the course countries, only the UK is a consolidated liberal democracy.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Mexico is accurate?

**A.** Mexico saw a dominant party lose the presidency in 2000 after more than 70 years in power. The PRI lost the presidency in 2000.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Russia is accurate?

**A.** Russia has a federal system. Mexico, Nigeria and Russia are federal; the UK, China and Iran are unitary.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Iran is accurate?

**A.** Iran is a theocratic republic in which a cleric holds the highest authority. Iran's Supreme Leader, a senior cleric, is the highest authority.

**Q.** Which of the following statements about Nigeria is accurate?

### Free-response practice: Comparative analysis: electoral systems for the legislature

Answer parts A, B and C, comparing Mexico and Nigeria.

**(a)** Define electoral systems for the legislature.

To earn the point: Accurate definition. Model answer: The rules that translate votes into legislative seats.

**(b)** Describe electoral systems for the legislature in Mexico and in Nigeria.

To earn the point: Accurate description for Mexico. Model answer: Mixed: 300 district seats and 200 proportional seats in the Chamber of Deputies.

To earn the point: Accurate description for Nigeria. Model answer: Single-member-district plurality for the House of Representatives.

**(c)** Explain a reason for the difference (or similarity) between Mexico and Nigeria with respect to electoral systems for the legislature.

To earn the point: A reason linked to regime type, history or political goals. Model answer: Countries choose rules to balance stable majorities with fair representation, or, in authoritarian systems, to advantage ruling parties.

### Free-response practice: Argument essay

Develop an argument as to whether economic liberalization promotes democratization.

In your response, you should do the following: respond with a defensible claim or thesis that establishes a line of reasoning; support your claim with at least two pieces of specific and relevant evidence from one or more course countries; use reasoning to explain why your evidence supports your claim; and respond to an opposing or alternative perspective using refutation, concession or rebuttal.

**(a)** Write your essay.

To earn the point: Thesis: a defensible claim that establishes a line of reasoning (1 point). Model answer: Economic liberalization does not necessarily promote democratization; regimes can liberalize markets while keeping political control.

To earn the point: Evidence: two pieces of specific, relevant evidence from course countries (up to 2 points). Model answer: China's market reforms since 1978 produced rapid growth while the CCP maintained one-party rule. Mexico's economic opening in the 1980s and 1990s coincided with growing political competition and the PRI's 2000 defeat.

To earn the point: Reasoning: explains why the evidence supports the claim (1 point). Model answer: Connects each example back to the thesis rather than just describing it.

To earn the point: Alternative perspective: responds with refutation, concession or rebuttal (1 point). Model answer: Some argue a growing middle class demands rights, as in Mexico; but China shows performance legitimacy can sustain authoritarian rule.
