Real GDP grows over the long run, but not smoothly. It rises faster than its trend in booms and falls below it in recessions. These swings are the **business cycle**.

## The four phases

| Phase | Real GDP | Unemployment | Inflation pressure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expansion | rising | falling | building |
| Peak | at its high point, often above potential | low | high |
| Contraction (recession) | falling | rising | easing |
| Trough | at its low point | high | low |

A **recession** is commonly described as at least two consecutive quarters of falling real GDP.

## Gaps

- **Recessionary (negative) gap:** actual output is below potential; cyclical unemployment exists.
- **Inflationary (positive) gap:** actual output is above potential; unemployment is below its natural rate and prices tend to rise.

## On the PPC

Recessions put the economy **inside** the PPC; long-run growth shifts the PPC **outward**. A boom can push output temporarily beyond potential (beyond the normal capacity line) by working resources unusually hard.

<Note title="Trend versus cycle">
Long-run economic growth (Unit 5) is about the trend line rising. The business cycle is about deviations from that trend. Policies to fight recessions (Unit 3) aim at the cycle, not the trend.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Identifying the gap">
Potential output is 20 trillion; actual real GDP is 18.5 trillion and falling, and unemployment is 7 percent with a natural rate of 4.5 percent. Describe the economy.

1. Actual is below potential: a recessionary gap of 1.5 trillion.
2. Unemployment exceeds the natural rate by 2.5 points: that is cyclical unemployment.
3. Real GDP is falling: the economy is in a contraction.

**Answer:** a contraction with a recessionary gap and cyclical unemployment.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

Potential real GDP is 3,000 billion, actual real GDP is 3,200 billion, the unemployment rate is 4.5% and the natural rate of unemployment is 5.5%. The economy is experiencing

**Answer:** an inflationary gap, with unemployment below its natural rate

**Why:** Output is above potential and unemployment is below the natural rate.

**Working:** Actual output is 200 billion above potential, and unemployment (4.5%) is below the natural rate (5.5%).

**Mistakes to avoid**

- a recessionary gap, with cyclical unemployment: Compare actual with potential output, and the unemployment rate with the natural rate.
- long-run equilibrium at full employment: Compare actual with potential output, and the unemployment rate with the natural rate.
- a recessionary gap, with unemployment below its natural rate: Compare actual with potential output, and the unemployment rate with the natural rate.
