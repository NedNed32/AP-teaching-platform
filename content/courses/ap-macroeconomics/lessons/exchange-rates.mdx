An exchange rate is just a price: the price of one money in terms of another. Reading it correctly is the first step in every Unit 6 question.

## Converting

If 1 dollar buys 0.80 euros, then 1 euro buys $$\frac{1}{0.80} = 1.25$$ dollars. The two rates are reciprocals.

## Appreciation and depreciation

When one currency appreciates, the other must depreciate.

| Change in euros per dollar | Dollar | Euro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rises (0.80 to 0.90) | appreciates | depreciates |
| falls (0.80 to 0.70) | depreciates | appreciates |

## Effects on trade

A stronger dollar makes US goods **more expensive** to foreigners and foreign goods **cheaper** to Americans: US exports fall, imports rise, and net exports fall. A weaker dollar does the opposite.

<Note title="Read the units">
"120 yen per dollar" is the price of a dollar in yen. When this number rises, the dollar is worth more. Always check which currency is in the denominator.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Pricing an import">
A British sweater costs 40 pounds. The exchange rate moves from 1 pound = 1.30 dollars to 1 pound = 1.20 dollars. What happens to its dollar price, and which currency appreciated?

1. Before: $$40 \times 1.30 = 52$$ dollars.
2. After: $$40 \times 1.20 = 48$$ dollars.
3. A pound buys fewer dollars: the pound depreciated and the dollar appreciated.

**Answer:** the sweater falls from 52 to 48 dollars; the dollar appreciated.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

The exchange rate is 4 aldos per gale. A good priced at 160 gales in Galen is sold in Aldoria. What is its price in aldos?

**Answer:** 640 aldos

**Why:** 160 × 4.

**Working:** 160 gales × 4 aldos per gale = 640 aldos.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- 40 aldos: Each gale is worth 4 aldos, so multiply.
- 160 aldos: The price must be converted at the exchange rate.
- 164 aldos: Currency conversion multiplies by the rate; it does not add.
