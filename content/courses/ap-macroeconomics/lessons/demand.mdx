Demand describes how much of a good buyers are willing and able to purchase at each price. It is half of the most-used model in economics.

## The law of demand

When the price of a good rises, people buy less of it, for three reasons:

- **Substitution effect:** other goods become relatively cheaper.
- **Income effect:** the same income buys less.
- **Diminishing marginal utility:** each extra unit adds less satisfaction, so people pay less for more.

## Movement versus shift

A change in the good's **own price** moves along the curve (a change in **quantity demanded**). Anything else that changes buying plans shifts the curve (a change in **demand**).

## Demand shifters

| Determinant | Increase in demand (shift right) when... |
| --- | --- |
| Tastes and preferences | the good becomes more popular |
| Number of buyers | the population of buyers grows |
| Income | income rises (normal good) or falls (inferior good) |
| Prices of related goods | a substitute's price rises, or a complement's price falls |
| Expectations | buyers expect a higher future price or income |

<Note title="Price cannot shift its own curve">
"The price rose, so demand fell" is a mistake: a price change moves along the curve. Reserve "demand fell" for a shift caused by a determinant.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Which way does demand shift?">
The price of gasoline rises sharply. What happens in the market for hybrid cars, and in the market for large SUVs?

1. Gasoline is a complement to cars that use a lot of it (SUVs): its higher price reduces demand for SUVs.
2. Hybrids save gasoline, so they become more attractive: demand for hybrids increases.

**Answer:** demand for hybrids shifts right; demand for SUVs shifts left.
</WorkedExample>

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** In the market for movie tickets, the price of streaming subscriptions (a substitute) rises. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of movie tickets?

**A.** Price will increase; quantity will increase. A pricier substitute increases demand. A pricier substitute increases demand: demand increases. A demand shift moves price and quantity in the same direction.

**Q.** In the market for smartphones, many new firms enter the market. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of smartphones?

**A.** Price will decrease; quantity will increase. More sellers increase supply. More sellers increase supply: supply increases. A supply shift moves price and quantity in opposite directions.

**Q.** In the market for sugar, the government places a per-unit tax on sugar producers. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of sugar?

**A.** Price will increase; quantity will decrease. A per-unit tax raises costs, decreasing supply. A per-unit tax raises costs, decreasing supply: supply decreases. A supply shift moves price and quantity in opposite directions.
