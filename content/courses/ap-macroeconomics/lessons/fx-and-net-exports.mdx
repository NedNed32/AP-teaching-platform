Exchange rates change what goods cost across borders, and so they change net exports, a component of aggregate demand.

## The chain

$$
\text{currency appreciates} \to \text{exports dearer, imports cheaper} \to X_n \text{ falls} \to AD \text{ left}.
$$

$$
\text{currency depreciates} \to \text{exports cheaper, imports dearer} \to X_n \text{ rises} \to AD \text{ right}.
$$

## Connecting monetary policy

Expansionary monetary policy lowers interest rates, which reduces foreign demand for domestic assets and depreciates the currency. That raises net exports, **adding** to the AD increase. The open-economy channel strengthens monetary policy.

## Connecting fiscal policy

Expansionary fiscal policy that raises interest rates appreciates the currency, which lowers net exports and **partly offsets** the AD increase.

<Note title="Who wins and loses">
A strong currency helps importers, tourists traveling abroad and consumers of foreign goods. It hurts exporters and domestic firms competing with imports.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="A full chain">
The central bank of Canada sells bonds. What happens to the Canadian interest rate, the Canadian dollar, Canadian net exports and Canadian AD?

1. Selling bonds reduces the money supply: interest rates rise.
2. Higher rates attract foreign funds: demand for Canadian dollars rises, so it appreciates.
3. Canadian exports become more expensive: net exports fall.
4. AD shifts left (reinforcing the contractionary policy).

**Answer:** interest rate up, Canadian dollar appreciates, net exports fall, AD decreases.
</WorkedExample>

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Estova's currency, the esto, depreciates significantly. What happens to Estova's net exports and aggregate demand?

**A.** Net exports will increase; aggregate demand will increase. Exports become cheaper abroad and imports more expensive. A weaker esto makes Estova's exports cheaper and imports pricier, so net exports rise and AD shifts right.

**Q.** Ferrin's currency, the ferro, appreciates significantly. What happens to Ferrin's net exports and aggregate demand?

**A.** Net exports will decrease; aggregate demand will decrease. Exports become more expensive abroad and imports cheaper. A stronger ferro makes Ferrin's exports pricier and imports cheaper, so net exports fall and AD shifts left.
