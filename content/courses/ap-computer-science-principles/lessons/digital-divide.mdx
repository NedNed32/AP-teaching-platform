Not everyone has the same access to computing. The **digital divide** is the gap between those with reliable access to devices and the Internet and those without.

## Causes

- Cost of devices and service.
- Geography: rural areas may lack fast connections.
- Age, disability, language and education.
- Government policies, including censorship.

## Effects

Access affects education, jobs, healthcare and civic participation. When schools, services or job applications move online, people without access fall further behind. The divide exists within countries and between them.

<Note title="Design choices matter">
Developers can narrow the divide by supporting older devices, low-bandwidth connections, accessibility tools and multiple languages.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Online-only services">
A city moves all permit applications online. Who is most likely to be harmed?

1. Residents without home Internet or with limited digital skills.
2. They lose easy access to a public service.

**Answer:** people on the disadvantaged side of the digital divide; offering an alternative would reduce the harm.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the facts and ideas the practice exams ask about this topic, each with the answer and the reason. If you can answer all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Facts and ideas the exams test

Cover the answers and test yourself.

**Q.** Machine learning finds patterns in medical data that help detect disease early, but the same data could reveal private health details. What does this illustrate?

**A.** Data analysis can have both beneficial and harmful effects. The same innovation can help and harm.

**Q.** Ride-sharing apps let drivers earn money on flexible schedules but may reduce income for traditional taxi drivers. What does this show?

**A.** An effect can be beneficial for some people and harmful for others. Effects of innovations are rarely only good or only bad.

**Q.** A social media platform built to help friends share photos is also used to spread false news quickly. This is an example of what?

**A.** An unintended harmful effect of a computing innovation. Creators did not plan this use.

**Q.** Which is an example of the digital divide?

**A.** Students in a rural area cannot complete online homework because they lack reliable Internet access. Unequal access to technology is the digital divide.

**Q.** A city moves all job applications online. Which group is most likely to be harmed?

**A.** People without home computers or Internet access. This can widen the digital divide.

**Q.** Which action would most help reduce the digital divide?

**A.** Providing free public Wi-Fi and devices at libraries. That increases access for people without it.
