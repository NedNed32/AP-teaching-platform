Detective controls produce evidence; someone must review it.

## Sources

- **Badge logs**: user, door, time, granted or denied.
- **Cameras** and motion sensors.
- **Door alarms** (forced open, held open).
- **Visitor logs**.

## Signs of an attack

- Access outside a person's normal hours or areas.
- Repeated denied attempts at a door.
- One badge used in two places impossibly close in time.
- Entries without a matching badge record (tailgating, seen on camera).
- Physical evidence: tampered locks, unknown devices plugged into ports.

<Note title="Correlate">
The strongest evidence combines sources: a badge entry at 2:14 a.m. plus camera footage plus a new device on the network.
</Note>

<WorkedExample title="Read the log">
A log shows badge 4471 denied at the server room 5 times between 23:10 and 23:14, then granted at 23:16. What should an analyst do?

1. Repeated failures followed by success is suspicious.
2. Check camera footage and confirm with the badge owner.

**Answer:** treat it as a possible intrusion: verify identity and review video.
</WorkedExample>

## On the practice exams

These are the kinds of questions the practice exams ask about this topic. Each has a full answer, the reasoning, and the reason each wrong option is wrong. If you can do all of them without looking, you are ready for this topic on the exams.

### Worked exam question 1

A security analyst reviews this door access log.

```text
08:16  badge 4471  Office 2      GRANTED
08:17  badge 6603  Lab B      GRANTED
08:18  badge 1042  Office 2      GRANTED
08:19  badge 2217  North Gate   GRANTED
08:19  badge 2217  South Gate   GRANTED
```

Which entry is most suspicious, and why?

**Answer:** Badge 2217, because it was used at two distant gates in the same minute, suggesting a cloned badge

**Why:** One badge cannot physically be in two distant places at once.

**Working:** Badge 2217 shows use at two distant gates at the same time; investigate with camera footage and the badge holder.

**Mistakes to avoid**

- Badge 4471, because it was granted access: Single, normal granted entries are expected.
- Badge 6603, because it was granted access: Single, normal granted entries are expected.
- Badge 1042, because it was granted access: Single, normal granted entries are expected.
